Week 8 · Seeking the Lost vs. Seeking Status

Tuesday – The Least of These

"I have decided to stick with love. Hate is too great a burden to bear." – Martin Luther King Jr.
Bible (ESV): Matthew 25:40 – "And the King will answer them, ‘Truly, I say to you, as you did it to one of the least of these my brothers, you did it to me.’"

Jesus said something amazing: when we help "the least of these," we are helping Him. Who are the least? They are the hungry, the thirsty, the stranger, the person without clothes, the sick, and the prisoner. They are the people the world overlooks. They are the ones with no status and no power. Jesus says that how we treat them is how we treat Him. That changes everything. Feeding a hungry stranger is not just a nice thing to do. It is serving Jesus Himself.

Dr. King decided to stick with love, even though hate would have been easier. Hate is a heavy burden. It poisons the person who carries it. Love sets us free to serve. A leader who seeks status spends time with important people who can do something for them. A servant leader spends time with "the least of these" who can do nothing for them in return. They visit the sick. They welcome the stranger. They feed the hungry. They do it with love, because they know they are serving Jesus. Look around your life this week. Who is the "least" near you? Who is hungry, lonely, or forgotten? Go to them with love. When you do, you are not just helping a person. You are meeting Jesus face to face.

Prayer:

King Jesus, You told us that when we serve the least, we serve You. Today, open my eyes to see You in the hungry man on the corner, the new stranger at church, the sick neighbor, the lonely widow. Take the heavy burden of hate out of my heart and fill it with love instead. I pray for every person who feels like "the least," unseen, unloved, forgotten. Let them know they are precious to You. Help me choose love every single day, and serve the least of these as if I were serving You. Amen.
