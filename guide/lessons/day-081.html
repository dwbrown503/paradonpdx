Week 17 · Forgiveness as a Leadership Tool

Monday – The Key That Unlocks

"Forgiveness is the key that unlocks the door of resentment and the handcuffs of hatred." — Corrie ten Boom
Bible (ESV): Ephesians 4:32 — "Be kind to one another, tenderhearted, forgiving one another, as God in Christ forgave you."

Corrie ten Boom knew about hatred. She watched the Nazis murder her sister in a prison camp. After the war, she traveled and spoke about forgiveness, and one night a former prison guard, one of the cruel men from her camp, walked up to her and asked for her forgiveness. Her hand froze. Everything in her wanted to refuse. But she prayed, she reached out her hand, and she forgave him. In that moment, she said she felt God's love flood through her like electricity. The key turned. The handcuffs fell off.

Resentment is like locking yourself in a prison and throwing away the key. You think you are punishing the person who hurt you, but you are the one stuck inside. Hatred is like handcuffs on your own wrists. Forgiveness is the key, and here is the surprise: the person it sets free first is you.

Ephesians 4:32 tells us to forgive each other just as God forgave us. Think about how much God has forgiven you. Every wrong, every selfish choice, every unkind word. He forgave it all through Christ. When that truth sinks in, forgiving others stops feeling impossible and starts feeling like the only natural response.

This calls us to be better people because a forgiving leader sets the whole team free. When you forgive quickly, your team learns it is safe to make mistakes and try again. Grudges poison a group from the inside, but forgiveness keeps the air clean. This week, look for a locked door in your heart. Turn the key. You will be amazed at how light you feel on the other side.

Prayer:

Father, I am holding a grudge, and it feels like handcuffs, heavy, cold, keeping me chained to the past. Today, help me use the key of forgiveness. I choose to release the family member who hurt me, the friend who betrayed me, the boss who wronged me. Unlock the door of my resentment and let Your peace walk in. I pray for everyone locked in hatred tonight. Turn their keys too. Show us that forgiving does not excuse the wrong. It frees the heart that was wronged. Amen.
