Week 8 · Seeking the Lost vs. Seeking Status

Monday – The Shepherd Who Searches

"What counts in life is not the mere fact that we have lived. It is what difference we have made to the lives of others that will determine the significance of the life we lead." – Nelson Mandela
Bible (ESV): Luke 15:4 – "What man of you, having a hundred sheep, if he has lost one of them, does not leave the ninety-nine in the open country, and go after the one that is lost, until he finds it?"

Jesus told a story about a shepherd with one hundred sheep. When one sheep wandered off and got lost, the shepherd did something surprising. He left the ninety-nine safe sheep and went searching for the one that was lost. He did not say, "Oh well, I still have ninety-nine." He went after the one until he found it. That is how God loves us. No person is too small, too far gone, or too unimportant to be searched for.

Nelson Mandela said that what counts in life is not just that we lived, but what difference we made in the lives of others. A servant leader thinks like the shepherd. They do not chase after important people to make themselves look good. They go after the lost ones: the lonely kid at school, the coworker everyone avoids, the neighbor who stopped coming to church, the family member who feels forgotten. Seeking status means asking, "Who can help me look important?" Seeking the lost means asking, "Who needs me most?" The shepherd's joy was not in keeping his ninety-nine safe. His joy was in finding the one. Your life becomes significant when you spend it searching for the lost and bringing them home.

Prayer:

Lord, my Shepherd, thank You for searching for me when I was lost. Today, give me a shepherd's heart. Show me the one who needs finding, the lonely coworker eating lunch alone, the teenager drifting from family, the neighbor nobody visits. Help me leave my comfort and go to them with kindness, not judgment. I pray for every lost sheep in the world, the addicted, the runaway, the forgotten. Send someone to search for them. Let my life count by the difference I make, and bring the lost ones home. Amen.
