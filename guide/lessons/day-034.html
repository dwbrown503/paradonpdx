Week 7 · The Parable of the Faithful Steward

Thursday – Ready When the Master Returns

"Silence in the face of evil is itself evil: God will not hold us guiltless. Not to speak is to speak. Not to act is to act." – Dietrich Bonhoeffer
Bible (ESV): Luke 12:43 – "Blessed is that servant whom his master will find so doing when he comes."

In Jesus' story, the master goes away for a while, and the servant does not know exactly when he will return. The faithful servant keeps working anyway. He does not take a break from doing good. He does not say, "The master is gone, so I can do whatever I want." He stays ready. Jesus told this story to teach us how to live every day. We do not know the future, but we know our Master. So we keep serving, keep loving, and keep doing what is right.

Dietrich Bonhoeffer lived this out in a terrible time. When evil was rising in his country, many people stayed silent because speaking up was dangerous. Bonhoeffer refused to be silent. He said that silence in the face of evil is evil itself. He stood up for what was right, even though it cost him his life. A servant leader stays ready by staying faithful. They do not wait for the perfect moment to do good. They do good now. They speak up when someone is being hurt. They act when they see a need. They do not let fear or laziness make them silent. Imagine if the Master returned today. Would He find you serving? That is the question that keeps a steward's heart awake and ready.

Prayer:

God, my Master, I want to be ready for You. Do not let me stay silent when I see someone treated badly at work or a neighbor in need. Wake up my heart every morning with a desire to serve. Help me do good now, not later. Make the call, write the note, lend the hand today. I pray for people suffering in silence, waiting for someone to speak up for them. Give me courage like Bonhoeffer had. When You return, Lord, may You find me feeding Your people and loving my neighbors. Amen.
