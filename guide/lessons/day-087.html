Week 18 · The Upper Room and the Basin (John 13)

Tuesday – Small Acts, Great Love

"Not all of us can do great things. But we can do small things with great love." — Mother Teresa
Bible (ESV): Galatians 5:13 — "For you were called to freedom, brothers. Only do not use your freedom as an opportunity for the flesh, but through love serve one another."

Mother Teresa spent her life caring for the poorest people in Calcutta, India. She did not build hospitals with her own hands or change governments. She held the hands of dying people. She fed hungry children one bowl at a time. She said the world does not need big heroes as much as it needs ordinary people doing small things with great love.

That is exactly what Jesus taught in the upper room. Washing feet was a small thing. It took a few minutes. But it was done with great love, and two thousand years later we are still talking about it. Small acts done with love have a way of growing bigger than we ever imagined.

Think about your own life. You may never preach to thousands or lead a big organization. But you can do small things today. You can text a friend who is going through a hard time and tell them you are thinking of them. You can make an extra plate of food for a neighbor. You can smile at the cashier and ask how their day is going. You can hold the door, give up your seat, or say thank you to someone who rarely hears it.

These things look tiny. But to the person receiving them, they can feel huge. A kind word can change the whole direction of someone's bad day. A small act of service tells a person, "You matter. You are seen. You are loved." That is what it means to serve one another through love. Do not wait until you can do something big. Start small, start today, and do it with all your heart.

Prayer:

God, You set me free, not so I can serve myself, but so I can serve others in love. Today, use my freedom for small acts with great love: the ride I offer, the meal I cook, the hour I give to help a neighbor move. When I am tempted to spend all my free time on me, redirect my feet. I pray for everyone who feels they have nothing to give. Show them their small acts matter. Let my freedom look like love in action, all day long. Amen.
