Friday – Hungry? Feed Them

"The best way to find yourself is to lose yourself in the service of others." — Mahatma Gandhi
Bible (ESV): Matthew 25:35 — "'For I was hungry and you gave me food, I was thirsty and you gave me drink, I was a stranger and you welcomed me,'"

Jesus told a story about the day He will judge the world. In the story, He said something amazing: when we feed someone who is hungry, it is like we are feeding Him. When we give a drink to someone who is thirsty, it is like we are giving it to Him. When we welcome a stranger, we are welcoming Him.

That means every act of kindness matters more than we think. The meal you share with a hungry neighbor is not just a meal. It is a gift to Jesus Himself. The water bottle you hand to someone on a hot day is not just water. It is love given to the Lord. Every small act of serving someone in need is a holy moment.

This week we have seen the whole picture. A boy shared his small lunch and thousands were fed. God loves a cheerful giver. There is always enough when God provides. And the church is meant to share like family. Now Jesus brings it all together: what you do for the hungry and the stranger, you do for Me.

So do not wait for a big moment. Look for the hungry person in your everyday life. Maybe it is a coworker who skips lunch. Maybe it is a neighbor who is too proud to ask. Maybe it is someone at a shelter downtown. Feed them. Welcome them. In serving them, you will find something precious — you will find Jesus, and you will find the best version of yourself. The world may tell you that finding yourself means looking inward. Jesus says the opposite is true. Lose yourself in serving others, and you will discover who you were truly made to be.

Prayer:

Lord Jesus, You said, "I was hungry and you gave me food." Today, do not let me explain that away. Show me the hungry person in my path, the homeless man at the intersection, the kid who never has lunch money, the senior choosing between food and medicine. Help me feed them, simply and quickly, the way I would feed You. I pray for every empty stomach tonight. Move my feet, open my wallet, and fill my hands. Let no one near me go hungry if I can help it. Amen.
