Week 18 · The Upper Room and the Basin (John 13)

Friday – Built for Others

"The church is the church only when it exists for others." — Dietrich Bonhoeffer
Bible (ESV): Mark 10:43-44 — "But it shall not be so among you. But whoever would be great among you must be your servant, and whoever would be first among you must be slave of all."

Dietrich Bonhoeffer was a German pastor who stood up against great evil during World War II. He believed that a church that only takes care of itself has forgotten what it is for. The church, he said, only truly becomes the church when it exists for others. The same is true for any team, any family, and any individual follower of Jesus. We are at our best when we are turned outward.

Think about the hospitality team at a church. The team brews coffee, sets out tea and water, greets people at the door, and prepares communion. None of those jobs are about the team members themselves. Every single task exists for someone else: the guest who is nervous about visiting, the tired parent who needs a warm smile, the lonely person who needs to feel welcome. When the team remembers it exists for others, even pouring coffee becomes an act of love.

This is the lesson of the basin. Jesus did not wash feet to build His own reputation. He did it for His friends. He was thinking of them, not Himself. When we live for others, we become more like Him.

Being better people to those around us starts with this simple shift: stop asking what the group can do for me, and start asking what I can do for the group. Stop asking what the church can give me, and ask whom the church can serve through me. A life built for others is a life built on the rock. It will stand, it will bless, and it will look like Jesus.

Prayer:

God, forgive me for making church, and my life, all about me: my preferences, my comfort, my growth. You said the church exists for others. Today, turn my eyes outward. Show me the visitor sitting alone in the back row, the new believer full of questions, the neighbor who has never heard Your name. Help me build my days around serving them. I pray for every church tempted to exist for itself. Wake us up. Make my life, like Yours, built for others. Amen.
