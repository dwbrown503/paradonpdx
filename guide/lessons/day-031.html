Week 7 · The Parable of the Faithful Steward

Monday – Faithful With Little

"Life's most persistent and urgent question is, 'What are you doing for others?'" – Martin Luther King Jr.
Bible (ESV): Luke 12:42-43 – "And the Lord said, ‘Who then is the faithful and wise manager, whom his master will set over his household, to give them their portion of food at the proper time? Blessed is that servant whom his master will find so doing when he comes.’"

Jesus told a story about a master who put one servant in charge of the whole house while he was away. The faithful servant did his job well, feeding everyone on time and taking care of things. Jesus said this servant would be blessed when the master came home. A steward is someone who takes care of something that belongs to someone else. We are all stewards. Our time, our money, our talents, and even our families are gifts from God, and He trusts us to take care of them.

Dr. King said the most urgent question in life is, "What are you doing for others?" That is the steward's question. A faithful steward does not wait for a big job to start serving. They are faithful with little things first. Do you take care of the small tasks at work, even when no one is watching? Do you keep your promises to your family? Do you use your money wisely? Do you treat your own body and health with care? A servant leader is faithful in the small, hidden jobs. That is how God knows He can trust them with bigger ones. Today, look at the little things in your life. Do them well. Feed the people God put in your care. The Master is watching, and He will be pleased.

Prayer:

Lord, everything I have belongs to You, my time, my money, my gifts, my family. They are on loan from Your hand. Today, help me do the small jobs well, even when no one is watching: the report done right, the kitchen cleaned, the promise kept. I pray for everyone who is hungry, waiting for someone to feed them on time. Make me faithful, someone who shows up and does what I said I would do. Teach me to ask every day, "What am I doing for others?" May You find me doing Your work with joy. Amen.
