Week 8 · Seeking the Lost vs. Seeking Status

Wednesday – Status Will Not Save You

"Injustice anywhere is a threat to justice everywhere." – Martin Luther King Jr.
Bible (ESV): Luke 19:10 – "For the Son of Man came to seek and to save the lost."

In Jesus' day, the religious leaders loved status. They loved the best seats, the respectful greetings, and the praise of the crowds. But they looked down on the poor, the sick, and the sinners. They thought they were close to God because they looked important. Jesus said they were wrong. He came to seek and to save the lost, not to hand out prizes to the proud. Status cannot save you. Only love can change you.

Dr. King wrote that injustice anywhere is a threat to justice everywhere. He meant that we cannot ignore suffering just because it is not happening to us. When we chase status, we start to ignore the hurting people around us. We walk past the homeless person. We avoid the difficult neighbor. We stay silent when someone is treated unfairly. But a servant leader does the opposite. They use whatever position they have to lift up the people at the bottom. They speak for those who have no voice. They remember that Jesus left heaven's glory to come find the lost. If the Son of God was willing to leave His high position to serve, surely we can let go of our small pride to help someone in need. Do not build your life on being important. Build it on seeking the lost.

Prayer:

Father, forgive me for the times I chased status instead of serving people, wanting the title, the recognition, the important seat. Today, help me let go of pride and love of being important. I pray for everyone suffering injustice, treated unfairly because of where they live, what they look like, or how much they have. Give me eyes to see their pain and courage to stand beside them. Remind me that Jesus left heaven to seek and save the lost. Make me a leader who does the same with whatever I have. Amen.
