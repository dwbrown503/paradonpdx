Week 22 · The Ransom Paid: Death as Ultimate Service

Friday – Leading From Behind

"It is better to lead from behind and to put others in front, especially when you celebrate victory when nice things occur." — Nelson Mandela
Bible (ESV): Philippians 2:5-8 — "Have this mind among yourselves, which is yours in Christ Jesus, who, though he was in the form of God, did not count equality with God a thing to be grasped, but emptied himself, by taking the form of a servant, being born in the likeness of men. And being found in human form, he humbled himself by becoming obedient to the point of death, even death on a cross."

We have reached the end of Week 22, and Mandela gives us the perfect summary. The best leaders do not push themselves to the front. They lead from behind. They put others in front. When something good happens, they let others take the bow. This is exactly what Jesus did. He was in the form of God, the highest position there is. But He did not cling to it. He emptied Himself, took the form of a servant, and humbled Himself to the point of death on a cross. The King of the universe led from behind all the way to Calvary.

Leading from behind is the opposite of how the world works. The world says to promote yourself, take the credit, and make sure everyone knows what you did. Jesus says to empty yourself, lift others up, and let God handle the recognition. This does not mean leaders are passive or weak. Mandela was not weak. Jesus was not weak. Leading from behind takes more strength than leading from the front, because it requires killing pride every single day.

Think about what this looks like for you. As a parent, it means celebrating your children's wins more than your own. As a team member, it means making your coworkers look good. As a church volunteer, it means being happy when someone else gets thanked. As a friend, it means listening more than talking. Each time you put someone else in front, you are thinking with the mind of Christ.

This is the ultimate demonstration of servant leadership. The cross is the final proof that greatness is found in giving, not getting. Carry this mind with you: empty yourself, serve others, and trust God with the rest. That is how Jesus led. That is how we lead too.

Prayer:

Lord, teach me to lead from behind, to put others in front, especially when good things happen. Today, when the team succeeds, let me celebrate them, not me. When someone else gets the praise, help me cheer the loudest. Empty me of the need to be first, the way Jesus emptied Himself. I pray for every leader tempted to take the credit tonight. Humble us. Show me the quiet joy of watching others shine, knowing I helped lift them there. Amen.
