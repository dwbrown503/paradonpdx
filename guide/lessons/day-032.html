Week 7 · The Parable of the Faithful Steward

Tuesday – Much Is Given, Much Required

"Pride gets no pleasure out of having something, only out of having more of it than the next man." – C.S. Lewis
Bible (ESV): Luke 12:48 – "But the one who did not know, and did what deserved a beating, will receive a light beating. Everyone to whom much was given, of him much will be required, and from him to whom they entrusted much, they will demand the more."

Jesus teaches a simple but serious rule: the more you have been given, the more is expected of you. If you have been given knowledge, you are expected to share it. If you have been given money, you are expected to be generous. If you have been given a position of leadership, you are expected to serve the people under your care. This is not a burden. It is an honor. God trusts you with much because He believes in you.

C.S. Lewis warned us about pride. He said pride is not about enjoying what you have. It is about enjoying having more than the next person. Pride turns blessings into weapons. A proud leader uses their gifts to feel better than others. A faithful steward uses their gifts to lift others up. Ask yourself: What has God given me? Maybe it is a good job, a strong body, a kind family, or a talent for music, cooking, or listening. Whatever it is, it is not just for you. It is a trust. Use it to feed people, the way the faithful servant fed the household. Do not compare yourself to others or brag about what you have. Instead, ask, "How can I use what God gave me to help someone today?" That is what a great steward does.

Prayer:

Father, thank You for every good gift You have given me, a home, work, health, people who love me. I know that much is expected from those who have received much. Today, show me how to share what I have: an extra meal for a hungry neighbor, money for someone who has very little, time for someone who is lonely. Take away my pride and my need to have more than others. Help me see my gifts as a trust from You, not trophies for myself. Show me how to lift others up today. Amen.
