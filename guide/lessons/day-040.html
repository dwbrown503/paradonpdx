Week 8 · Seeking the Lost vs. Seeking Status

Friday – If You Can't Feed a Hundred, Feed One

"If you can't feed a hundred people, then feed just one." – Mother Teresa
Bible (ESV): Matthew 25:40 – "And the King will answer them, ‘Truly, I say to you, as you did it to one of the least of these my brothers, you did it to me.’"

Sometimes the needs of the world feel too big. There are millions of hungry people, millions of lonely people, and millions of hurting people. It is easy to feel small and give up before you even start. Mother Teresa felt that too. She worked among the poorest people in the world, and the need never ended. But she said, "If you can't feed a hundred people, then feed just one." She knew that changing the whole world starts with loving one person.

Jesus agrees. He said that whatever we do for one of the least of these, we do for Him. Notice He said "one." Not a hundred. Not a thousand. One. A servant leader does not wait until they can fix everything. They start with the one person in front of them. You cannot feed a hundred people today. But you can buy lunch for one hungry person. You cannot visit every lonely person in your city. But you can call one lonely friend. You cannot end all suffering. But you can sit with one suffering person and listen. Do not let the size of the problem stop you from doing the small thing. God takes our small acts of love and multiplies them in ways we cannot see. Feed one. Love one. Serve one. That is how the world changes.

Prayer:

Lord, the needs of the world feel so big, and I feel so small. But You tell me to start with one. Today, show me the one person You want me to love this week, the hungry coworker, the lonely senior, the tired mom. Help me stop waiting until I can do something big, and do the small thing today. I pray for the millions of hungry, lonely, hurting people across the earth. Raise up servants to feed them, one by one. Bless my small acts of kindness and multiply them for Your glory. Amen.
