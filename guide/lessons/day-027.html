Week 6 · The First Shall Be Last (Mark 9 & 10)

Tuesday – The Last Shall Be First

"Kind words can be short and easy to speak, but their echoes are truly endless." – Mother Teresa
Bible (ESV): Mark 9:35 – "And he sat down and called the twelve. And he said to them, ‘If anyone would be first, he must be last of all and servant of all.’"

The disciples were arguing about which one of them was the greatest. They were walking with Jesus, the Son of God, and instead of learning from Him, they were fighting over first place. Jesus sat down, called them close, and said something shocking: "Anyone who wants to be first must be the very last, and the servant of all." He turned the whole idea of winning upside down. In God's kingdom, you do not climb over people to get to the top. You bend down to lift people up.

Mother Teresa understood this. She said kind words are short and easy to speak, but their echoes never end. A kind word is a small thing. It costs nothing. But it can change a person's whole day, or even their whole life. When a leader chooses to be last, they give away the spotlight so others can shine. They speak kind words to the person nobody notices. They thank the quiet worker. They encourage the struggling student. The world says, "Push yourself to the front." Jesus says, "Step to the back and serve." When you serve others first, God lifts you up in His own way and in His own time. The last become first, not by fighting for it, but by loving their way there.

Prayer:

Dear Father, I confess that I often want to be first. I want the praise, the best seat, and my name called out. Forgive me. Today, help me give away the spotlight. Let someone else go first in line, let a coworker take credit for a good idea, and speak a kind word to the person everyone overlooks. I pray for every person who feels forgotten today. Remind them that You see them. Teach me the way of Jesus, who chose the last place so He could serve us all. Make me a leader who lifts others up. Amen.
