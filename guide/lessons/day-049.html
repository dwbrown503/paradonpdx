Week 10 · Elevating Women and Children in Ancient Culture

Thursday – "Listening Like Mary"

"The first service that one owes to others in the fellowship consists in listening to them." — Dietrich Bonhoeffer
Bible (ESV): Luke 10:39 — "And she had a sister called Mary, who sat at the Lord's feet and listened to his teaching."

Jesus came to visit two sisters, Martha and Mary. Martha got busy. She cooked, she cleaned, she set the table. There was much to do, and she was doing it all. Mary did something very different. She sat at the Lord's feet and listened to what He said.

In that time and place, this was shocking. Rabbis taught men, not women. A woman sitting at a teacher's feet was taking the place of a student, a disciple. But Jesus did not send her away. He welcomed her. He let her learn. When Martha complained that Mary was not helping in the kitchen, Jesus said Mary had chosen the better thing. He lifted Mary up and honored her place at His feet.

Bonhoeffer wrote that the first service we owe others is listening to them. Not fixing them. Not lecturing them. Listening. Real listening is one of the rarest gifts in the world. It says, "You matter enough for me to stop and hear you."

Think about real life. A coworker is upset and starts talking, and we jump in with advice before they finish. A child tells a long, rambling story, and we check our phone. A spouse says, "We need to talk," and we brace ourselves instead of opening our hearts. We mean well. But we are being Martha when someone needs us to be Mary.

Listening looks like this. Put the phone down. Turn your body toward the person. Let them finish. Ask one gentle question, like "How did that feel?" or "Tell me more." Do not rush to fix it. Sometimes people do not need answers. They need to be heard.

Mary sat. She listened. And Jesus called it the better thing. When we truly listen to a child, a coworker, a neighbor, or a friend, we are giving them what Jesus gave Mary: dignity, honor, and a place at the feet of love.

Prayer:

Father, forgive me for how often I listen with half my heart, nodding while my eyes are on my phone. Today, teach me to listen like Mary listened: fully, quietly, with love. When my spouse, my child, or my friend needs to talk, help me put everything down and give them my whole attention. Show me that listening is a real way to serve. Quiet my urge to fix and rush. Let the people I love walk away knowing they were truly heard today. In Jesus' name, Amen.
