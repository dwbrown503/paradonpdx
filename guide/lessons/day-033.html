Week 7 · The Parable of the Faithful Steward

Wednesday – Working for God, Not People

"Often when you think you're at the end of something, you're at the beginning of something else." – Fred Rogers
Bible (ESV): Colossians 3:23-24 – "Whatever you do, work heartily, as for the Lord and not for men, knowing that from the Lord you will receive the inheritance as your reward. You are serving the Lord Christ."

Have you ever worked hard at something and nobody noticed? Maybe you cleaned the house and no one said thank you. Maybe you did your best at work and the boss gave the credit to someone else. It hurts. It is tempting to stop trying. But the Bible gives us a secret that changes everything: we are not really working for people. We are working for God. When you wash the dishes, you are serving Christ. When you do your job with care, you are serving Christ. When you help your neighbor, you are serving Christ.

Fred Rogers reminded us that endings are often beginnings in disguise. When your hard work goes unnoticed, that is not the end of the story. God sees it, and He is writing a bigger story with your faithfulness. A servant leader does not work for applause. They work for God. That means they do their best even on the boring days, even on the hard days, even when no one is watching. People will notice the difference. They will see someone who is steady, honest, and full of joy, and they will want to know why. Your quiet faithfulness is a sermon without words. Keep going. God is keeping score, and His reward is better than any praise from people.

Prayer:

Lord Jesus, thank You for seeing the work that no one else sees. I pray for every tired worker today, the janitors mopping floors late at night, the cooks, the caregivers, the parents up with a crying baby. Strengthen their hands and their hearts, and remind them their work matters to You. Help me to work with all my heart, not for applause, but for You. When I feel unnoticed, fill me with Your joy. Let my quiet faithfulness be a light that points others to You. Amen.
