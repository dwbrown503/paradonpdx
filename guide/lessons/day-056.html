Monday – A Boy's Small Lunch

"We cannot all do great things, but we can do small things with great love." — Mother Teresa
Bible (ESV): John 6:9 — "'There is a boy here who has five barley loaves and two fish, but what are they for so many?'"

One day Jesus was teaching a huge crowd of people. It was getting late and everyone was hungry. Thousands of people were there, and there was almost no food. But one boy had packed a small lunch: five little loaves of bread and two small fish. It was not much at all. Still, he gave it up so it could be shared.

Jesus took that little lunch, gave thanks, and broke the bread. Then something amazing happened. The food kept going and going. There was enough to feed the whole crowd, and some was even left over. That boy's small lunch became a big meal for thousands of people.

This is how serving works. You do not need to have a lot. You just need to give what you have with a loving heart. Maybe you only have one extra sandwich. Maybe you only have an hour to help at a food pantry. Maybe you only have a bag of cans from your shelf. Give it anyway. Small gifts in God's hands grow into big blessings.

When we hold back because what we have seems too small, we miss the joy of sharing. But when we offer it with love, God can do more with it than we could ever imagine. Never think your gift is too little. In a world where people go hungry every day, a small act of kindness can be the start of something big. And you never know whose heart will be touched. That boy probably never dreamed his little lunch would feed a crowd. But he gave it anyway, and Jesus did the rest. You can do the same.

Prayer:

Lord, thank You for the boy who gave his small lunch, five loaves and two fish, and watched You feed thousands. Today, help me offer what little I have: the extra sandwich in my bag, the few dollars in my wallet, the hour I could spare. When I think "this is too small to matter," remind me that nothing is too small in Your hands. Bless the givers, the sharers, the packers of lunches. Take my small offering and multiply it into more love than I can imagine. Amen.
