Week 9 · Touching the Untouchable (Leper Cleansing)

Wednesday – Thankful Hearts, Healed Lives

"We shall never know all the good that a simple smile can do." — Mother Teresa
Bible (ESV): Matthew 8:3 — "And Jesus stretched out his hand and touched him, saying, 'I will; be clean.' And immediately his leprosy was cleansed."

The moment Jesus touched the man, everything changed. The sickness that had stolen his home, his family, and his place in the community was gone. But notice something beautiful. The healing did not only clean his skin. It gave him his life back. He could hug his children again. He could walk into town without shouting "unclean." He could worship with his friends again. Kindness does not just fix a problem. It gives a person their dignity back. A simple smile can do the same kind of work in smaller ways. Think about a tired cashier who has heard complaints all day long. A warm smile and a thank you can remind her that she matters. Think about the new volunteer at church who does not know anyone yet. A smile and a kind hello can turn a scary first day into a happy one. Think about the neighbor who just lost his wife. Your smile across the fence can tell him he is not forgotten. We may never know all the good a smile can do, because we rarely see the whole story. The person we smiled at goes home feeling a little lighter, and they are kinder to the next person they meet. That is how healing spreads. Jesus touched the untouchable man, and his life was made whole. When we share small, kind moments with the people others overlook, we become part of that same healing. A thankful heart notices the good in others. A healed life passes the healing on. That is the chain we get to be part of every single day.

Prayer:

Dear God, thank You for the way Jesus touched the man no one else would touch and made his life whole. Thank You for the small gifts of kindness You put in my hands each day, a smile for the tired cashier, a kind word for the lonely neighbor, a helping hand for the overwhelmed mom. Give me a thankful heart that notices the good in people. Remind me I never know how far a small act of love can travel. Let my words and my smile bring a little of Your healing to the world today. Amen.
