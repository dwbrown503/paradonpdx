Week 20 · The Trials: Silence Under False Accusation

Tuesday – Courage at the Testing Point

"Courage is not simply one of the virtues but the form of every virtue at the testing point." — C.S. Lewis
Bible (ESV): Matthew 26:63 — "But Jesus remained silent. And the high priest said to him, 'I adjure you by the living God, tell us if you are the Christ, the Son of God.'"

There is a moment in every virtue when it gets tested. Love is tested when someone is unlovable. Patience is tested when everything goes wrong. And courage is tested when staying quiet costs you everything. C.S. Lewis said courage is what every virtue looks like at its testing point. Jesus showed this during His trial. Falsely accused, surrounded by enemies, He remained silent. That silence took more courage than any speech.

We often think courage means speaking up loudly. Sometimes it does. But sometimes the bravest thing is to stay quiet when every part of you wants to argue, defend, and explain. Jesus knew who He was. He did not need the high priest's approval. He did not need to win the argument. He trusted His Father's plan, and that trust gave Him the strength to be still.

In real life, we face smaller testing points every day. Someone criticizes our work unfairly. A family member misunderstands our motives. A coworker takes credit for what we did. In those moments, we have a choice: explode with angry words, or respond with quiet strength. Not every accusation deserves an answer. Not every criticism needs a defense. Sometimes the most powerful response is calm trust.

This calls us to be better people because it teaches us self-control. Words spoken in anger can wound people for years. Silence rooted in trust can disarm a conflict. When we learn to be still like Jesus, we become safe people for others to be around. We show them that our worth does not depend on winning every argument, but on belonging to God.

Prayer:

God, I am at my testing point, accused, misunderstood, pressured to defend myself with angry words. Like Jesus standing silent before His accusers, give me the courage to stay steady. Keep my voice calm and my heart clean. Help me trust that You see the truth even when no one else does. I pray for everyone falsely accused today. Be their defender. Teach me that sometimes the bravest thing is not to argue, but to stand quiet and let You fight for me. Amen.
