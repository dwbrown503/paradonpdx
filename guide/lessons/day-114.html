Week 23 · The Resurrection: Vindicated Humility

Thursday – Small Kindnesses, Big Victory

"Do your little bit of good where you are; it's those little bits of good put together that overwhelm the world." — Desmond Tutu
Bible (ESV): James 4:10 — "Humble yourselves before the Lord, and he will exalt you."

Desmond Tutu once said that when we each do our little bit of good, all those little bits put together can overwhelm the world. Think about that. You do not have to change the whole world by yourself. You just have to do your part, right where you are.

The Bible says, "Humble yourselves before the Lord, and he will lift you up." This is a promise. God sees the person who serves quietly. He sees the mom who gets up early to pack lunches. He sees the worker who does his job well when nobody is watching. He sees the teenager who is kind to the kid everyone else ignores. None of these acts make the news, but God notices every one of them.

Jesus lived this way. He spent most of His life as a carpenter in a small town, unknown to the world. When He finally became famous, He still chose the low road — washing feet, touching lepers, eating with people others looked down on. Then God lifted Him up higher than anyone, raising Him from the dead. Vindicated humility means God honors the lowly. The way down is the way up.

So what is your little bit of good today? Maybe it is a phone call to someone who is lonely. Maybe it is sharing your lunch. Maybe it is cleaning up a mess you did not make. Do it without telling anyone. Do it without posting about it. Do it for God and for the person in front of you.

You might feel small some days, like your kindness does not matter. But the resurrection says it does. Every small act of love is a seed, and God grows seeds into something big. Keep planting. He will do the lifting, in His time and in His way.

Prayer:

Lord, You promise to lift up those who humble themselves. Today, my victory will not come from being the loudest or the toughest. It will come from small kindnesses: the encouraging text, the held door, the sincere apology. When I want to fight for my rights, help me surrender them to You instead. I pray for everyone humbling themselves in hard places today. Exalt them in Your time. Let my small kindnesses be the weapons that win Your way. Amen.
