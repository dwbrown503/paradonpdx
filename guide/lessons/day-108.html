Week 22 · The Ransom Paid: Death as Ultimate Service

Wednesday – A Life That Counts

"What counts in life is not the mere fact that we have lived. It is what difference we have made to the lives of others that will determine the significance of the life we lead." — Nelson Mandela
Bible (ESV): 1 John 3:16 — "By this we know love, that he laid down his life for us, and we ought to lay down our lives for the brothers."

Nelson Mandela spent the best years of his life in prison, yet his life counted for more than most. Why? Because he measured his life not by how long he lived or how much he owned, but by the difference he made in the lives of others. Jesus is the perfect example. His earthly life lasted only about 33 years, but no life has ever made a bigger difference. He laid down His life for us, and that one act changed the world forever.

John writes that because Jesus laid down His life for us, we ought to lay down our lives for others. Most of us will not die as martyrs. But we can lay down our lives in daily pieces. We lay down our time when we help someone who needs us. We lay down our comfort when we serve in hard places. We lay down our pride when we admit we are wrong. We lay down our plans when God interrupts them with someone else's need. Piece by piece, day by day, we give our lives away, and that is what makes a life count.

Here is the question that calls us to be better people: when your life is over, what will have counted? Not your bank account. Not your follower count. Not how comfortable you were. What will count is the difference you made in the lives of others. The meals served, the lonely visited, the young mentored, the hurting comforted, the truth spoken in love. A life poured out for others is never wasted. It echoes into eternity. Jesus proved it. Mandela proved it. Now it is our turn.

Prayer:

Lord, what will count at the end of my life is not what I collected, but what difference I made. Today, help me lay down my time for others, the afternoon given to a lonely senior, the evening spent mentoring a teen, the lunch hour shared with a struggling friend. When I am tempted to measure my day by productivity, measure it by love instead. I pray for everyone wondering if their life matters. Show them it does. Make my life count where it counts most. Amen.
