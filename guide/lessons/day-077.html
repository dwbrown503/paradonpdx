Week 16 · Inhabiting Truth and Grace

Tuesday – No Time to Judge

"If you judge people, you have no time to love them." — Mother Teresa
Bible (ESV): John 8:32 — "and you will know the truth, and the truth will set you free."

Mother Teresa spent her days picking up dying people from the streets of Calcutta. She did not ask how they ended up there. She did not sort them into good and bad. She just loved them. She knew a secret: judging and loving cannot live in the same heart at the same time. Every minute you spend judging someone is a minute you are not loving them.

Jesus said the truth will set you free, and one of the greatest truths is this: judging others puts you in prison, not them. When you judge, your heart gets hard. You start keeping a list of everyone's faults. You feel better than them for a moment, but then you feel lonely, because judges do not have friends, they have courtrooms. But when you let the truth set you free from judging, your hands are suddenly free to help and your heart is free to love.

Servant leaders are tempted to judge all the time. The team member who is always late. The neighbor who never says thank you. The family member who made the same mistake again. Truth matters, and we should not pretend problems do not exist. But there is a difference between seeing a problem clearly and judging a person's worth. You can address the lateness with kindness. You can set a boundary with love.

This calls us to be better people because the world has enough judges and not enough lovers. When you put down the gavel and pick up a towel, like Jesus washing feet, people feel it. They open up. They try harder. They change, not because you shamed them, but because you loved them. Trade your judging time for loving time today.

Prayer:

Lord, forgive me for the minutes I waste judging people, sizing up the neighbor's yard, whispering about a coworker's choices, deciding who is worthy. That time could be spent loving them instead. Today, when judgment rises in me, replace it with love. Help me know the truth that sets free: that every person is precious to You. I pray for everyone crushed under others' judgment today. Lift their heads. Make me someone whose presence feels like grace, not a courtroom. Amen.
