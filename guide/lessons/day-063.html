Week 13 · Rebuking Religious Exploitation (Cleansing the Temple)

Wednesday – The Greatest Will Serve

"Do not wait for leaders; do it alone, person to person." — Mother Teresa
Bible (ESV): Matthew 23:11 — "'The greatest among you shall be your servant.'"

In Jesus' time, religious leaders loved to look important. They wanted the best seats, the grand titles, and the praise of the people. But Jesus turned the whole idea of greatness upside down. He said, "The greatest among you will be your servant." In God's kingdom, greatness is not about being served. It is about serving.

Think about that. The world says: climb higher, get more, be the boss. Jesus says: bend lower, give more, be the helper. The greatest person in the room is not the one everyone waits on — it is the one who quietly helps everyone else. That is servant leadership. It is leading by serving, not by bossing.

Mother Teresa said, "Do not wait for leaders; do it alone, person to person." You do not need a title to serve. You do not need permission to be kind. You can start right where you are, with the person right in front of you. Visit the lonely neighbor. Help the new coworker learn the job. Carry the groceries for the tired mom. Small acts, done in love, change the world one person at a time.

This is also how we stand against the kind of exploitation Jesus confronted in the temple. The money changers used religion to lift themselves up. Servants use their lives to lift others up. When we choose to serve, we refuse to play the power game. We say no to using people, and yes to loving them.

Ask yourself today: who can I serve? It does not have to be big. A servant's heart shows up in small, faithful choices. Wash the dishes without being asked. Listen to someone who needs to talk. Give your time without keeping score. Jesus, the greatest of all, washed His friends' feet. If He was willing to serve, we can be too. Greatness is not a throne. Greatness is a towel.

Prayer:

Lord, You said the greatest among us shall be our servant. Today, do not let me wait for leaders, committees, or perfect plans. Help me serve person to person, right where I am. Show me the one I can help this morning: the discouraged friend, the overwhelmed parent, the lonely elder. Give me the humility to do the lowly task with a glad heart. I pray for everyone waiting to be noticed by someone important. Let them be found by an ordinary servant like me. Make my life great in Your way. Amen.
