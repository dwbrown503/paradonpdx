Week 9 · Touching the Untouchable (Leper Cleansing)

Friday – Loved So We Can Love

"The greatest thing that we can do is to help somebody know that they're loved and capable of loving." — Fred Rogers
Bible (ESV): John 13:34-35 — "'A new commandment I give to you, that you love one another: just as I have loved you, you also are to love one another. By this all people will know that you are my disciples, if you have love for one another.'"

All week we have been learning about Jesus and the man with leprosy. Now we come to the reason behind it all. Jesus did not touch the man because the man earned it. He touched him because love was in his heart, and love has to move. Then Jesus gave us a new command. Love one another the way I have loved you. Notice the order. First we are loved. Then we love. We do not love people to earn God's favor. We love people because God's love is already filling us up, and a full heart has to pour out somewhere. Think of it like a cup under a running faucet. When the cup is full, the water spills over the sides. When God's love fills us, it spills over onto everyone around us. That is why the greatest thing we can do is help someone know they are loved. Maybe you know a neighbor who believes nobody cares about her. Your kindness can be the first drop of proof that love is real. Maybe there is a teenager at church who acts tough because he is afraid no one sees him. Your attention can show him he matters. Maybe there is a stranger on the bus who looks tired and worn down. Your smile can tell him he is not invisible. When people feel loved, something wakes up inside them. They begin to believe they are capable of loving too. That is the miracle. Love does not run out when we give it away. It multiplies. And Jesus said the world would know we belong to him by our love. Not by our buildings. Not by our arguments. By our love. That is our mark. That is our mission.

Prayer:

Loving God, thank You for loving me first, long before I earned it or deserved it. Fill my heart so full of Your love that it spills over onto everyone I meet today. Help me be the proof of Your love to the neighbor who feels forgotten, the teenager who feels unseen, the new kid at school who feels invisible. Make me gentle, patient, and kind in all I do, at home, at work, in the checkout line. Let the world see that I belong to You by the way I love. Amen.
