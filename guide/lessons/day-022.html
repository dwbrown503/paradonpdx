Week 5 · The Beatitudes as Leadership Traits (Matthew 5)

Tuesday – Hungry for What Is Right

"Not all of us can do great things. But we can do small things with great love." – Mother Teresa
Bible (ESV): Matthew 5:6 – "Blessed are those who hunger and thirst for righteousness, for they shall be satisfied."

Think about how hungry you feel when you skip a meal. Your stomach growls and your mind cannot stop thinking about food. Jesus says we should want goodness and fairness in the world that badly. He calls it hungering and thirsting for righteousness. A servant leader does not just want to be good for their own self. They want what is right for everyone, and they want it so much that it drives them to act.

Mother Teresa fed hungry people and cared for the sick in the streets of Calcutta. She could not fix the whole world, but she never stopped doing small things with great love. Each small act came from a big hunger for what is right. You can do the same. When you see someone treated unfairly at school or work, do not look away. Stand up for them. When a neighbor needs help carrying groceries, help them. When you see a problem in your community, ask how you can help fix it. Greatness in Jesus' eyes is not about big titles or big stages. It is about a heart that cannot rest while wrong things are happening. The more you feed that hunger by doing good, the more God fills your heart with joy and purpose. Your small acts of love are food for a hungry world.

Prayer:

Heavenly Father, I come to You hungry for what is right. When I see a coworker treated unfairly or a kid at school being picked on, do not let me walk past and pretend I did not see. Give me courage to speak up with kindness and stand beside the one who is hurting. Bless the quiet helpers in my town who feed the hungry and visit the lonely. Use my small hands and my small voice today. Keep my heart restless until wrong things are made right, and fill me with joy as I serve. Amen.
