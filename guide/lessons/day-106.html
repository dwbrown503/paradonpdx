Week 22 · The Ransom Paid: Death as Ultimate Service

Monday – Called to Die to Self

"When Christ calls a man, he bids him come and die." — Dietrich Bonhoeffer
Bible (ESV): Mark 10:45 — "For even the Son of Man came not to be served but to serve, and to give his life as a ransom for many."

Those are strong words from Bonhoeffer: when Christ calls us, He calls us to die. He did not mean we all die physically like Jesus did. He meant we die to ourselves. We die to selfishness, to pride, to the constant demand to have our own way. Jesus showed us what this looks like. He came not to be served but to serve, and He gave His life as a ransom for many. A ransom is the price paid to set someone free. Jesus paid with His life to set us free.

Dying to self sounds scary, but it is actually the doorway to the best life. Think of a seed. A seed must fall into the ground and die before it can grow into a plant. If it refuses to die, it stays a lonely little seed forever. In the same way, when we hold tightly to our own way, our own comfort, and our own plans, our lives stay small. When we let those things die, God grows something beautiful in us.

What does dying to self look like in real life? It looks like giving up your right to be first. It looks like forgiving when you would rather stay angry. It looks like serving when you would rather rest. It looks like telling the truth when a lie would be easier. Each time we choose God's way over our own way, a little piece of selfishness dies, and a little more of Jesus comes alive in us.

This calls us to be better people because it frees us from the prison of self. The most miserable people are the ones who think only of themselves. The most joyful people are the ones who have died to self and live for God and others. Jesus paid the ransom. Now He invites us to live the ransomed life.

Prayer:

Lord, when You call me, You bid me come and die, die to my selfish plans, my demand for comfort, my need to be in charge. Today, help me die a little: give up my seat, my schedule, my right to be right. When self screams, let Your Spirit whisper louder. I pray for everyone clinging to self today. Show us the freedom on the other side of surrender. Teach me that dying to self is not losing life. It is finally finding it. Amen.
