Week 3 · Secure Identity as a Prerequisite for Service

Wednesday – Ordinary People, God-Sized Purpose

"Everybody can be great, because everybody can serve. You don't have to have a college degree to serve. You don't have to make your subject and your verb agree to serve. You only need a heart full of grace. A soul generated by love." — Dr. Martin Luther King Jr., "The Drum Major Instinct" sermon, February 4, 1968
Bible (ESV): "For we are his workmanship, created in Christ Jesus for good works, which God prepared beforehand, that we should walk in them." — Ephesians 2:10

Dr. Martin Luther King Jr. once preached that everybody can be great, because everybody can serve. You do not need a college degree. You do not need perfect grammar. You do not need money or fame. You only need "a heart full of grace" and "a soul generated by love." That is wonderful news, because it means no one is disqualified from serving — not you, not anyone.

Many people quietly believe that serving is for special people — pastors, missionaries, heroes. The rest of us feel too ordinary. Too young. Too old. Too broken. Too busy. But the Bible tells a different story: "For we are his workmanship, created in Christ Jesus for good works, which God prepared beforehand, that we should walk in them." The word "workmanship" means a work of art, shaped by hand. You are God's handmade creation. And before you were even born, God prepared good works for you to do — works that fit exactly who He made you to be.

Notice the order of things. God did not say, "Do great things, and then I will love you." He said, "I made you, I love you, and I already prepared a place for you to serve." Your identity comes first. Your service flows out of it like a river flows from a spring.

When you truly believe this, comparison loses its power over you. You stop measuring yourself against the person with the bigger gift or the louder voice. A grandmother praying quietly for her grandchildren is doing a prepared good work. A teenager mowing an elderly neighbor's lawn is doing a prepared good work. A friend who simply shows up and listens is doing a prepared good work.

You do not have to be impressive. You just have to be willing. God already made you enough — now go and be His hands in a world that needs them.

Prayer:

Creator God, thank You for shaping me with Your own hands — Your handiwork, made on purpose and for a purpose. When I scroll through my phone and feel too ordinary next to everyone else's gifts, remind me that You prepared good works for me before I was even born. Take away my habit of comparing myself to the person with the louder voice or the bigger gift. Give me a heart that serves with grace and a soul that overflows with love, and open my eyes to simple ways I can serve today — a prayer for my family, a hand for my neighbor. My real, everyday life is exactly where You want to use me. In Jesus' name, amen.
