Week 23 · The Resurrection: Vindicated Humility

Monday – God Lifts Up the Lowly

"Not all of us can do great things. But we can do small things with great love." — Mother Teresa
Bible (ESV): Philippians 2:8–9 — "And being found in human form, he humbled himself by becoming obedient to the point of death, even death on a cross. Therefore God has highly exalted him and bestowed on him the name that is above every name."

Humility means not making yourself the most important person in the room. It means putting others first and being okay with nobody noticing. Jesus is the greatest picture of this. He was God's own Son, but He came to earth as a servant. He washed His friends' feet. He let people hurt Him. He died on a cross, even though He had done nothing wrong. That is what the verse above is talking about. But the story does not end there. God raised Jesus from the dead and gave Him the highest place of all. That is what "vindicated humility" means: God showed that Jesus was right all along by lifting Him up.

This is good news for us. You do not have to push your way to the top. You do not have to brag, argue, or prove you are important. When you quietly do the right thing — helping a neighbor, forgiving someone who hurt you, doing a hard job without complaining — God sees it. He may not lift you up the way the world lifts people up, but He honors a humble heart.

Living this out looks simple. It looks like letting someone else take the credit. It looks like saying, "I'm sorry," first. It looks like choosing the small job nobody wants, like Mother Teresa said, and doing it with great love. It can feel hard when others get noticed and you don't. But the resurrection tells us the truth: humble love wins in the end. God never forgets the servant. Keep serving with a soft heart, and trust Him to do the lifting.

Prayer:

Father, You humbled Yourself and then God lifted You up, and You promise to lift the humble too. Today, I choose the low place: the last seat, the unseen task, the quiet apology. When the world tells me to promote myself, help me trust Your promotion instead. I pray for every lowly, overlooked person tonight, the dishwasher, the nursing aide, the single mom. Lift their heads. Teach me that the way up in Your kingdom is down, and help me walk it gladly. Amen.
