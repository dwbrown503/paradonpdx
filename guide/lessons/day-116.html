Week 24 · Restoring the Fallen Leader (Peter on the Beach)

Monday – Jesus Comes to the Shore

"Forgiving is not forgetting; it's actually remembering — remembering and not using your right to hit back. It's a second chance for a new beginning." — Desmond Tutu
Bible (ESV): John 21:4 — "Just as day was breaking, Jesus stood on the shore; yet the disciples did not know that it was Jesus." John 21:7 — "That disciple whom Jesus loved therefore said to Peter, ‘It is the Lord!’ When Simon Peter heard that it was the Lord, he put on his outer garment, for he was stripped for work, and threw himself into the sea."

Think about what Peter had just done. A few days earlier, he had told three different people, "I don't even know Jesus." He was broken. He went back to fishing, back to the old life, probably sure Jesus would never want him again. But Jesus did not wait for Peter to come crawling back. Jesus walked out to the shore in the early morning and made breakfast. He came to Peter first.

That is what real forgiveness looks like. As Desmond Tutu said, forgiveness is not pretending the hurt never happened. It is remembering, and still choosing not to strike back. It is giving someone a second chance at a brand-new beginning.

When a leader falls, the world expects them to be finished. One big mistake and people say, "They're done." But Jesus does the opposite. He shows up at the shoreline of their life with food and warmth. Notice He did not start with a lecture. He started with breakfast. Care came before correction.

This teaches us something huge about how we treat people who have failed. We do not wait for them to fix themselves first. We go to them. We bring kindness first. If someone around you has messed up — a friend, a coworker, a family member — do not wait for them to earn their way back. Go to their shore. Show up. Offer a warm meal and a warm heart before you offer advice. That is how Jesus restores, and that is how He wants us to restore. A servant leader does not keep score of failures. A servant leader keeps showing up.

Prayer:

Lord Jesus, like You stood on the shore cooking breakfast for disciples who had failed You, come to my shore today. I messed up, the harsh words, the missed chance, the promise broken, and I feel like hiding. But You come close instead of condemning. Meet me in my ordinary morning: the kitchen, the commute, the quiet. I pray for everyone hiding in shame tonight. Walk onto their shore. Remind us that failure is never the end when You show up. Amen.
