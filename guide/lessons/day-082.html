Week 17 · Forgiveness as a Leadership Tool

Tuesday – Stick With Love

"I have decided to stick with love. Hate is too great a burden to bear." — Martin Luther King Jr.
Bible (ESV): Matthew 6:14-15 — "For if you forgive others their trespasses, your heavenly Father will also forgive you, but if you do not forgive others their trespasses, neither will your Father forgive your trespasses."

Dr. King had every reason to hate. He was beaten, jailed, threatened, and finally murdered for his work. People spit on him and called him terrible names. But he made a decision, not a feeling, but a decision: "I have decided to stick with love." He understood that hate is not just wrong. It is heavy. It is a burden too great to bear. Hate wears you down from the inside until you cannot lead, cannot love, and cannot live in peace.

Forgiveness is how we stick with love. It is the daily decision to put down the heavy bag of hate and pick up the lighter load of love. Jesus makes this very serious in Matthew 6. He says if we forgive others, our Father will forgive us. But if we refuse to forgive, we block our own forgiveness. That is not God being mean. It is God showing us how forgiveness works: it has to flow through us, not just to us. A clogged pipe cannot carry water.

Servant leaders cannot afford to carry hate. You are trying to lift other people up, and you cannot lift anyone while you are weighed down yourself. The volunteer who betrayed your trust. The friend who talked behind your back. The family member who never apologized. Put down those bags, one by one, through forgiveness.

This calls us to be better people because people who stick with love change the temperature of every room they enter. They bring peace instead of tension. They bring hope instead of bitterness. Decide today, like Dr. King did: I will stick with love, no matter what. It is lighter, it is freer, and it is the way of Jesus.

Prayer:

Lord, I have decided to stick with love, but some days hate feels easier. When the insult replays in my mind and revenge sounds sweet, remind me that hate is too heavy a burden to carry. Today, help me lay it down. If I forgive others, You forgive me. Let that promise steady my shaking heart. I pray for everyone choosing between love and hate today. Tip the scales toward love. Keep my feet on love's path, even when it climbs uphill. Amen.
