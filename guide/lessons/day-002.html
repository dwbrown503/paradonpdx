Week 1 · The Prophetic Profile (Isaiah's Servant Songs)

Tuesday – Called and Chosen for a Purpose

"I alone cannot change the world, but I can cast a stone across the waters to create many ripples." — Mother Teresa
Bible (ESV): "The LORD called me from the womb, from the body of my mother he named my name. ... I will make you as a light for the nations, that my salvation may reach to the end of the earth." — Isaiah 49:1, 6

Isaiah 49 gives us the second Servant Song, and it begins with something amazing: God called the Servant before he was even born. From his mother's womb, God had a plan for his life and a name for him. That tells us something wonderful about you too. You were not an accident. God knew you before you were born, and He made you for a reason. Mother Teresa said, "I alone cannot change the world, but I can cast a stone across the waters to create many ripples." One small stone can send ripples farther than you can see. In the same way, God told the Servant, "It is too small a thing for you to only help one group of people. I will make you a light for the nations, so that my salvation may reach to the end of the earth." One faithful servant became a blessing for the whole world. You may feel small. You may think your life does not matter much. But God loves to use small, faithful people to do big things. The meal you share with a lonely neighbor, the patient words you give your child, the honest work you do when no one is watching — these are stones dropped into water. You may never see all the ripples. A kind word you say today might lift someone who then lifts someone else, and on and on. Servant leaders do not wait until they feel important. They serve right where they are, trusting God to carry the ripples farther than they can reach. This week, remember that you were called before you were born, and drop your stone with joy.

Prayer:

Dear Father, thank You for calling us before we were ever born and giving each of us a name. When we feel too small to matter, remind us that one little stone can send ripples farther than we can see. Help us drop our stones with joy — the meal we share with the lonely neighbor next door, the patient words we give our child at bedtime, the honest work we do when nobody is watching. We may never see how far the ripples go, but You do. Use our small acts of love to carry Your light to people who need it most. In Jesus' name, Amen.
