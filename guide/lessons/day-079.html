Week 16 · Inhabiting Truth and Grace

Thursday – A Threat to Justice Everywhere

"Injustice anywhere is a threat to justice everywhere." — Martin Luther King Jr.
Bible (ESV): Ephesians 4:15 — "Rather, speaking the truth in love, we are to grow up in every way into him who is the head, into Christ,"

Dr. King wrote these words in a letter from a jail cell in Birmingham, Alabama. People told him to stay in his own city and mind his own business. Why care about injustice somewhere else? His answer changed history: injustice anywhere is a threat to justice everywhere. If it is wrong in one place, it is wrong in every place. We are all connected.

This is truth and grace working together. Truth says: name the wrong clearly. Do not soften it, do not look away. Grace says: speak it in love, the way Paul teaches in Ephesians 4:15. Speaking the truth in love means your goal is never to destroy a person. Your goal is to heal a situation and help everyone grow, including the one doing wrong.

Servant leaders live this out in small, everyday ways. When you hear a cruel joke about someone at work, truth says that joke is wrong. Grace says you can speak up kindly instead of attacking the joker. When you see a volunteer being treated unfairly, truth says it needs to stop. Grace says you can defend them without hating the offender. You become a protector, not a punisher.

This calls us to be better people because silence in the face of wrong is not kindness, it is fear wearing a mask. Real love speaks up. But real love also speaks gently. When you learn to speak the truth in love, you become the kind of person people trust with hard things. They know you will tell them the truth, and they know you will do it with a caring heart. That is a servant leader worth following.

Prayer:

Lord, give me the courage to speak the truth in love. Today, when I see something wrong at work, in my family, or in my community, do not let me stay silent just to keep the peace. Teach me to speak gently but clearly, correcting without crushing, honest without harsh. Injustice anywhere threatens justice everywhere, even in my own small corner. I pray for everyone afraid to speak up today. Loosen our tongues with love. Make my words a bridge to what is right. Amen.
