Week 7 · The Parable of the Faithful Steward

Friday – Gifts Meant to Be Given

"Yesterday is gone. Tomorrow has not yet come. We have only today. Let us begin." – Mother Teresa
Bible (ESV): 1 Peter 4:10 – "As each has received a gift, use it to serve one another, as good stewards of God’s varied grace."

God has given every person at least one gift. Maybe you are good at cooking. Maybe you are a good listener. Maybe you can fix things, or sing, or make people laugh, or organize a messy room. These gifts are not accidents. The Bible says they are God's grace in different forms, and we are stewards of them. A gift that is never used is like a lamp that is never lit. It cannot help anyone in the dark.

Mother Teresa said we only have today, so let us begin. Do not wait until you feel ready. Do not wait until you have more time or more money. Start now, with what you have, where you are. A servant leader looks at their gifts and asks, "Who needs this today?" The cook makes a meal for a sick neighbor. The listener sits with a lonely friend. The fixer helps an elderly person with a broken door. The singer brings joy to a nursing home. Every gift becomes greater when it is given away. That is the beautiful secret of stewardship: the more you give, the more you have. Your gifts were never meant to sit on a shelf. They were meant to be placed in the hands of people who need them. Begin today.

Prayer:

Generous God, thank You for the gifts You placed in each of us. Today, help me stop waiting for tomorrow and start serving today, with my cooking, my listening ear, my strong back, my cheerful words. I pray for everyone who feels like they have nothing to offer. Show them the beautiful gifts You hid inside them. Teach me to see my talents as Your grace to share, not my own to keep. Bless every person who uses their gifts to feed the hungry, comfort the lonely, and lift up the hurting. Amen.
