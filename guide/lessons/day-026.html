Week 6 · The First Shall Be Last (Mark 9 & 10)

Monday – Greatness Is Service

"Everybody can be great... because anybody can serve. You don't have to have a college degree to serve. You don't have to make your subject and your verb agree to serve. You only need a heart full of grace. A soul generated by love." – Martin Luther King Jr.
Bible (ESV): Mark 10:43-45 – "But it shall not be so among you. But whoever would be great among you must be your servant, and whoever would be first among you must be slave of all. For even the Son of Man came not to be served but to serve, and to give his life as a ransom for many."

One day, two of Jesus' disciples asked Him for the best seats in heaven. They wanted to be important. Jesus told them that in His kingdom, greatness works backwards. The way up is down. The way to be first is to be last. The way to be great is to serve. This is one of the most important lessons Jesus ever taught. He said that even He, the Son of God, came not to be served. He came to serve and to give His life for us.

Dr. King said the same thing in his own words. He told a church full of people that anybody can be great, because anybody can serve. You do not need money. You do not need a fancy degree. You do not need a big title. All you need is a heart full of grace and a soul full of love. Think about the people who have helped you most in life. They were probably not famous. They were probably people who served you quietly: a parent who worked hard, a teacher who stayed late, a friend who listened. That is greatness. A servant leader does not ask, "Who will serve me?" They ask, "Who can I serve today?" When you live this way, your life becomes great in God's eyes, even if the world never notices.

Prayer:

Lord Jesus, thank You for showing us what true greatness looks like. You washed dirty feet, and You gave Your life for us. Today, when I am tempted to chase a title or a bigger name tag, remind me that greatness is found in serving. Show me the small, unseen jobs, like the dishes in the sink, the trash that needs taking out, or the neighbor who needs a ride, and help me do them with joy. I pray for everyone who feels small and unimportant today. Let them know how much they matter to You. Amen.
