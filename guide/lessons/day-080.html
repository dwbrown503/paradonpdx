Week 16 · Inhabiting Truth and Grace

Friday – Love Until It Hurts

"I have found the paradox, that if you love until it hurts, there can be no more hurt, only more love." — Mother Teresa
Bible (ESV): Romans 5:8 — "but God shows his love for us in that while we were still sinners, Christ died for us."

Mother Teresa discovered a strange secret about love. Most people love until it starts to cost them something, and then they stop. They love until someone is ungrateful, then they quit. They love until it is inconvenient, then they walk away. But she kept loving past the point of pain, and she found that on the other side of the hurt there was not emptiness. There was more love.

God showed us this first. Romans 5:8 says that while we were still sinners, while we were still running from Him, ignoring Him, and breaking His heart, Christ died for us. He did not wait until we were good enough. He did not wait until we said thank you. He loved until it hurt, all the way to the cross, and that love changed the world forever.

Servant leaders are called to this same costly love. You will pour into people who never thank you. You will forgive people who hurt you again. You will serve people who do not even like you. It will hurt sometimes. But Mother Teresa promises that if you keep going, the hurt turns into more love, not less. Your heart stretches. Your compassion deepens. You start to love like God loves.

This calls us to be better people because comfortable love changes nothing, but costly love changes everything. The people around you do not need a leader who loves them only when it is easy. They need a leader who stays when it is hard. Love until it hurts this week, and watch God fill the hurt places with even more love to give away.

Prayer:

God, loving is easy when people are kind, but You call me to love until it hurts. Today, help me love the difficult person: the relative who criticizes, the coworker who takes credit, the neighbor who complains. When my love runs out, fill me with Yours, the love that died for sinners, including me. Take away my limits and my scorekeeping. I pray for everyone hard to love today; surround them with patient hearts. Let there be no more hurt in me, only more love. Amen.
