Week 25 · The Great Commission: Leading by Multiplying Servants

Monday – The Commission Is to Make Makers

"Not all of us can do great things. But we can do small things with great love." — Mother Teresa
Bible (ESV): Matthew 28:19-20 — "Go therefore and make disciples of all nations, baptizing them in the name of the Father and of the Son and of the Holy Spirit, teaching them to observe all that I have commanded you. And behold, I am with you always, to the end of the age."

Before Jesus went back to heaven, He gave His followers one big job. It is called the Great Commission. He did not say, "Go and gather a crowd." He said, "Go therefore and make disciples." A disciple is not just someone who watches. A disciple is someone who learns and then does. Jesus wants us to teach others to do the things He taught us: to love people, to serve people, to forgive people, and to welcome people. And here is the most important part. Jesus did not ask us to make followers of ourselves. He asked us to make followers of Him. A good leader does not build a fan club. A good leader builds more servants. Think of it this way. If you teach ten people to serve with love, and each of them teaches ten more, soon hundreds of people are serving. That is called multiplying servants, and it is exactly how Jesus spread His love. You do not have to do great things to be part of this. You can do a small thing with great love. You can show a new volunteer how to brew the coffee. You can stand beside someone at the door and teach them how to greet people with a warm smile. You can explain how to set out the communion cups with care. When you teach someone else to serve, you are living out the Great Commission right where you are. The love of Jesus grows every time one servant helps raise up another servant.

Prayer:

God, Your commission is not just to make followers, but to make makers, people who make more servants. Today, show me who I can invest in: the younger coworker, the new volunteer, my own child. Teach me to share what I know, model what I believe, and then step back and let them lead. When I am tempted to do it all myself, remind me that multiplying is Your math. I pray for every mentor pouring into someone today. Bless their investment. Make me a maker of servants. Amen.
