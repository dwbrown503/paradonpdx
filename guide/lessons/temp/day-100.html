Week 20 · The Trials: Silence Under False Accusation

Friday – The Silent Lamb

"Don't raise your voice, improve your argument." — Desmond Tutu
Bible (ESV): Isaiah 53:7 — "He was oppressed, and he was afflicted, yet he opened not his mouth; like a lamb that is led to the slaughter, and like a sheep that before its shearers is silent, so he opened not his mouth."

Hundreds of years before Jesus was born, the prophet Isaiah described Him as a lamb led to slaughter, silent before its shearers. On the day of His trials, that prophecy came true. Jesus was oppressed, and He was afflicted, yet He opened not His mouth to defend Himself. Like a lamb, He was quiet. Not because He was weak, but because He was strong enough to trust His Father completely.

Desmond Tutu's words fit this moment in a surprising way. Jesus did not raise His voice. He did not need to. His whole life was the argument. His love, His miracles, His kindness to the poor, His forgiveness of sinners, all of it spoke louder than any courtroom speech could. When the truth of who you are is clear, you do not need to shout.

There is a deep dignity in this kind of silence. It says, "I know who I am. I know who my Father is. I do not need your verdict to tell me my worth." Most of us get our sense of worth from what people think of us. So when people think badly of us, we panic and start shouting our defense. Jesus shows us a better foundation: our worth comes from God, and no false accusation can take it away.

This calls us to be better people by teaching us restraint and confidence. Restraint, because we learn to control our tongues when we are attacked. Confidence, because we learn that God's opinion of us matters more than anyone else's. A person who is secure in God's love does not need to win every argument. Like the silent Lamb, they can be still, and let their life do the talking.

Prayer:

Lord, like a lamb before its shearers, Jesus opened not His mouth. Today, when the argument heats up and my voice wants to rise, help me stay silent, not sulking, but peaceful. Improve my argument by improving my love. When I am tempted to win with words, teach me to win with gentleness. I pray for every home filled with shouting tonight. Bring quiet. Show me that silence under pressure is not defeat; it is the strength of the Lamb living in me. Amen.
