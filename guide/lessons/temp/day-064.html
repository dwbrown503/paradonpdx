Week 13 · Rebuking Religious Exploitation (Cleansing the Temple)

Thursday – Learning to Do Right

"A good head and a good heart are always a formidable combination." — Nelson Mandela
Bible (ESV): Isaiah 1:17 — "learn to do good; seek justice, correct oppression; bring justice to the fatherless, plead the widow's cause."

God spoke these words through the prophet Isaiah to people who looked very religious on the outside. They went to the temple. They offered sacrifices. They said all the right prayers. But God was not pleased. Why? Because outside the temple, they ignored the people who needed help. They did not defend the oppressed. They did not care for the fatherless or the widow. Their worship was empty because their love was empty.

Then God gave them a clear command: "learn to do good; seek justice, correct oppression; bring justice to the fatherless, plead the widow's cause."

Notice the first word: learn. Doing right is something we learn. Nobody is born knowing how to stand up for others. We learn it step by step, the way we learn to read or ride a bike. We practice. We make mistakes. We try again. God is patient with us as we learn.

What does it look like to learn to do right? It means we seek justice — we want things to be fair for everyone, not just for us. It means we defend the oppressed — when someone is being pushed down, we stand beside them. It means we take up the cause of the fatherless — we care for children who have no one to protect them. And we plead the case of the widow — we speak up for those who have no voice.

Nelson Mandela said, "A good head and a good heart are always a formidable combination." Doing right needs both. A good head helps us understand what is fair and wise. A good heart moves us to act with love. Together, they make us strong for others.

You do not have to fix the whole world today. Start small. Learn one person's story. Speak up one time. Help one family. God sees every small act of justice, and He is pleased.

Prayer:

God, You told us to learn to do good, to seek justice, correct oppression, and defend the fatherless. Today, be my teacher. Show me the kid with no dad who needs a mentor, the widow who needs her porch fixed, the worker being cheated who needs someone to speak up. Give me a good head and a good heart, working together. Forgive me for the times I knew what was right and did nothing. Make my hands quick to help and my voice brave for the voiceless. Amen.
