Week 14 · Prayer and Solitude as Fuel for Service

Tuesday – Small Worries Belong in Big Prayers

"Any concern too small to be turned into a prayer is too small to be made into a burden." — Corrie ten Boom
Bible (ESV): Philippians 4:6 — "do not be anxious about anything, but in everything by prayer and supplication with thanksgiving let your requests be made known to God."

Do you ever carry little worries around all day? The parking ticket you forgot to pay. The kind word you forgot to say. The friend you have not called back. They are small, but they pile up on your heart until they feel heavy. Corrie ten Boom learned this truth in the hardest place imaginable. She and her family hid Jewish people from the Nazis during World War II, and she was sent to a prison camp for it. She learned there that no worry is too small to hand to God, and no worry is too small to weigh you down if you keep it to yourself.

Servant leaders carry a lot. You worry about the people you serve, the team you lead, and whether you are doing enough. If you do not pray about those worries, they turn into stress, and stress turns into treating people badly. You snap at a volunteer. You stop listening to a hurting person. Your heart gets tired.

But here is the beautiful part: God wants every one of those small concerns. Nothing is too little for Him. When you pray about the small stuff, you are not bothering God. You are trusting Him. And a leader who trusts God with small worries becomes a calm, kind leader that people want to follow.

This calls us to be better people because prayer makes us lighter. When your heart is light, you smile more, you forgive faster, and you have room to care about other people's burdens instead of drowning in your own. Give God the small things, and watch how big your heart becomes.

Prayer:

Lord, I bring You my small worries today, the bill I am not sure I can pay, the doctor's appointment I am nervous about, my child's bad day at school. No concern is too small for prayer, and none is too big for You. Instead of carrying these alone until my shoulders ache, I lay them at Your feet. Replace my anxious thoughts with Your peace. I pray for every worried heart tonight. Teach us all to trade our burdens for prayer, one small worry at a time. Amen.
