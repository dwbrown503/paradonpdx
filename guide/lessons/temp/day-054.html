Week 11 · Crossing Racial Boundaries (The Samaritan Woman)

Thursday – "Good News for the Oppressed"

"Injustice anywhere is a threat to justice everywhere." — Martin Luther King Jr.
Bible (ESV): Luke 4:18 — "'The Spirit of the Lord is upon me, because he has anointed me to proclaim good news to the poor. He has sent me to proclaim liberty to the captives and recovering of sight to the blind, to set at liberty those who are oppressed,'"

Before Jesus began His public work, He stood up in the synagogue in His hometown and read from the prophet Isaiah. It was His mission statement — the reason He came. Listen to what He chose: good news to the poor. Freedom for the prisoners. Recovery of sight for the blind. Freedom for the oppressed. He did not say He came for the rich, the comfortable, or the powerful. He came for the people at the bottom — the ones the world had stepped on and forgotten.

Jesus also reminded His listeners of two stories where God sent His prophets to outsiders: a widow in Sidon and a man with leprosy from Syria — neither of them Jewish. The crowd was so angry they tried to throw Him off a cliff. God's love crosses racial lines, and they did not want to hear it.

Martin Luther King Jr. said, "Injustice anywhere is a threat to justice everywhere." We cannot say we love justice while ignoring the suffering of people who are different from us. If one group is held down, all of us are made smaller by it. The wall that traps them also traps a piece of our own humanity.

So what does this mean for us? Servant leaders lift up the people others push down: serving a meal at a shelter, visiting someone in prison, tutoring a child who is falling behind, or helping a refugee family learn a new city. Closer to home, it means noticing the coworker who always gets the worst shifts and asking why, refusing to laugh at jokes that punch down, and using your voice when someone else's is not heard.

Jesus did not just preach to the oppressed. He ate with them and touched them. He treated their pain as His own business. When we get close to hurting people and stand beside them, we are doing exactly what Jesus said He came to do. That is good news with skin on.

Prayer:

Lord Jesus, You came with good news for the oppressed, freedom for the captive, sight for the blind, hope for the crushed. Today, bring Your good news through me. Show me the coworker crushed by debt, the neighbor worn down by grief, the teenager who feels trapped. Give me words of hope and hands ready to help. I pray for everyone treated unfairly in our world. Use my small life to carry Your big promise: that no one is too far gone and no chain is too strong for You. Amen.
