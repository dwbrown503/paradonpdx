Wednesday – Enough for Everyone

"Live as if you were to die tomorrow. Learn as if you were to live forever." — Mahatma Gandhi
Bible (ESV): Matthew 14:20 — "And they all ate and were satisfied. And they took up twelve baskets full of the broken pieces left over."

When Jesus fed the five thousand, something wonderful happened at the end. The verse says they all ate and were satisfied. Nobody went home hungry. Nobody got only a crumb. Every single person had enough. And on top of that, there were twelve baskets full of food left over.

That is how God provides. When He is in charge of the sharing, there is always enough. In fact, there is more than enough. Think about a time you worried there would not be enough food for everyone at a table. Then people started passing dishes, and somehow everyone ate their fill. God's math is different from ours. What looks too small becomes plenty in His hands.

This is good news for a world where many people do not have enough to eat. God does not want anyone left out. He wants every person fed and cared for. And He often uses us to make that happen. When we share our food with a neighbor, volunteer at a shelter kitchen, or fill a food pantry shelf, we become part of His way of making sure everyone is satisfied.

Do not be afraid that giving will leave you with nothing. God's story ends with baskets full of leftovers. There is enough for everyone, and there is enough for you too. Trust Him, share freely, and watch how far His provision goes. When fear whispers that you cannot afford to give, remember those twelve baskets. God does not run short. He never has, and He never will. Your kindness today is safe in His hands.

Prayer:

Lord, thank You for the miracle of enough, baskets left over after everyone had eaten their fill. Today, quiet my fear that there will not be enough: not enough money, not enough time, not enough to share. Teach me to trust Your provision and to open my hands. Show me the leftovers in my life, the extra food, the spare room, the free Saturday, and who needs them. I pray for every hungry person tonight. Use Your people to make sure there is enough for everyone. Amen.
