Week 17 · Forgiveness as a Leadership Tool

Wednesday – Taught to Love

"No one is born hating another person because of the color of his skin, or his background, or his religion. People must learn to hate, and if they can learn to hate, they can be taught to love, for love comes more naturally to the human heart than its opposite." — Nelson Mandela
Bible (ESV): Colossians 3:13 — "bearing with one another and, if one has a complaint against another, forgiving each other; as the Lord has forgiven you, so you also must forgive."

Nelson Mandela spent nearly three decades locked in prison by people who hated him because of his skin color. When he walked free, the whole world held its breath. Would he seek revenge? Would he call his people to hate back? Instead, he called them to forgive. He believed something beautiful: no baby is born hating. Hate has to be taught. And if hate can be taught, then love can be taught too, because love comes more naturally to our hearts.

This is one of the most powerful leadership tools in the world. A leader who forgives teaches everyone watching how to forgive. When Mandela forgave the people who stole 27 years of his life, he taught a whole nation to choose love over revenge. Your forgiveness works the same way on a smaller stage. When you forgive the coworker who wronged you, your team learns that revenge is not the way. When you forgive a family member, your children learn that love is stronger than hate.

Paul tells us in Colossians to forgive as the Lord forgave us. That is the standard. Not forgive when it is easy. Not forgive when they deserve it. Forgive the way Jesus forgave you: fully, freely, and first. He forgave you before you even asked.

This calls us to be better people because every act of forgiveness is a lesson in love for someone watching. You may never know who is learning from you. A young person might be watching how you handle betrayal. A hurting friend might be watching how you handle pain. Teach them well. Show them that love comes naturally to the human heart, and forgiveness is how we set it free.

Prayer:

God, nobody is born hating. Hate is learned, which means love can be learned too. Today, teach me. When irritation rises toward someone different from me, replace it with curiosity, patience, and kindness. Help me bear with the annoying coworker, the difficult in-law, the stranger I do not understand. Forgive my complaints and my cold shoulder. I pray for every heart trained in hatred. Retrain us all in love. Make my life living proof that people really can change. Amen.
