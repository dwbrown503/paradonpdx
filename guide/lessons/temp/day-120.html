Week 24 · Restoring the Fallen Leader (Peter on the Beach)

Friday – Restoring Others Gently

"The first service one owes to others in a fellowship consists of listening to them." — Dietrich Bonhoeffer
Bible (ESV): Galatians 6:1 — "Brothers, if anyone is caught in any transgression, you who are spiritual should restore him in a spirit of gentleness. Keep watch on yourself, lest you too be tempted."

Jesus restored Peter on the beach. But the story does not end there. Peter went on to restore others. In the book of Acts, he preached, he prayed for the sick, he lifted up the broken, and he led thousands of people to Jesus. The man who once fell became a man who helped others get back up. That is the full circle of grace: we are restored so that we can restore.

The apostle Paul tells us exactly how to do it. In Galatians 6:1, he says that when someone is caught in sin, we should restore him in a spirit of gentleness. Gently. Not loudly. Not with gossip. Not with a lecture. Gently — the way Jesus handled Peter, with breakfast first and kindness all the way through.

Dietrich Bonhoeffer, a pastor who stood up for what was right even when it cost him his life, said that the first service we owe to others is listening to them. Before we speak, before we advise, before we correct — we listen. Listening is the first act of restoration. When someone has fallen, the most healing thing we can do is sit down, be quiet, and truly hear them.

So how do we restore a fallen friend? First, we listen. We let them tell their story without interrupting. Second, we remember our own failures — we stay humble, because Paul warns us that we could fall too. Third, we speak hope, not shame. We tell them what Jesus told Peter: your failure is not the end, and there is still work for you to do. Fourth, we walk with them. We do not just say "good luck" and walk away. We stay.

This is servant leadership at its finest. A servant leader does not throw broken people away. A servant leader picks them up gently, the way Jesus picked up Peter, and helps them find their feet again. This week we learned that Jesus restores the fallen. Now the question is: who around you needs restoring — and will you be the one to listen first?

Prayer:

Lord, someone I know has fallen, caught in a mistake, crushed by shame. Teach me to restore them gently, the way You restored Peter: no lectures, no gossip, just breakfast on the beach and a question about love. Help me listen first, speak softly, and guard their dignity. Keep me from pride, because I could be them. I pray for every fallen one tonight. Send them a gentle restorer. Make me the safe person people can run to and confess to. Amen.
