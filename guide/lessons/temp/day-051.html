Week 11 · Crossing Racial Boundaries (The Samaritan Woman)

Monday – "Tearing Down the Wall"

"My humanity is bound up in yours, for we can only be human together." — Desmond Tutu
Bible (ESV): Ephesians 2:14 — "For he himself is our peace, who has made us both one and has broken down in his flesh the dividing wall of hostility"

Picture a city with a tall wall down the middle. On one side live people who look like you and sound like you. On the other side live people who look different and speak in a different way. The wall keeps the two sides apart. No one crosses over. No one shares a meal. No one learns the other person's name. That wall is not only stone and brick. It is fear. It is old habit. It is the quiet thought that says, "They are not like us, so they must matter less."

Long ago, God's people and the other nations were separated in the same way. The temple in Jerusalem even had a real stone wall. Jewish people could worship on one side. Everyone else could only stand far off and watch. The wall told them they did not belong.

But Jesus came to knock that wall down. He did not add a door or make it shorter. He destroyed it. Our verse says He made the two groups one. His love is bigger than every line we draw between people.

Think of Jesus at the well in John 4. Jews did not speak to Samaritans. Men did not speak alone with women they did not know. Jesus broke both rules in one warm conversation. He asked a Samaritan woman for a drink of water, and her whole town found hope because of it.

Our walls today are harder to see, but they are just as real. They appear when we only spend time with people who look like us. They appear when we cross the street to avoid someone. They appear when we let a hurtful joke about someone's background pass by without a word.

You can help tear down a wall this week. Learn the name of a neighbor who comes from a different place. Share a meal together. Ask about their story and truly listen. When we step across the line, we discover what the wall was hiding all along: a fellow human being, made in the image of God, just like us.

Prayer:

God of peace, You tore down the wall between us and made us one. Today, help me tear down the walls in my own heart, the suspicion toward people who look different, talk different, or come from a different place. Give me the courage to befriend someone outside my usual circle: share a meal, learn their story, listen with respect. I pray for our divided neighborhoods and workplaces. Bind our humanity together the way You bound Yours to ours. Make me a builder of bridges, not walls. Amen.
