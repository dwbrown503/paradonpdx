Week 15 · Handling Criticism and Popularity

Wednesday – Impossible Until It's Done

"It always seems impossible until it's done." — Nelson Mandela
Bible (ESV): John 15:18 — "If the world hates you, know that it has hated me before it hated you."

Nelson Mandela spent 27 years in prison for standing against apartheid, the cruel system that separated people by the color of their skin in South Africa. The world told him change was impossible. Critics mocked him. Even some of his own people lost hope. But he kept going, and one day the impossible was done. He walked out of prison and later became the president of a free South Africa.

Servant leaders often feel the same way. You try to bring people together and someone criticizes you. You try to help the poor and someone says it will never work. You try to forgive and someone calls you weak. The voices of criticism can make the mission feel impossible. But Jesus warned us about this ahead of time. He said, "If the world hates you, keep in mind that it hated me first." Criticism is not a sign that you are failing. Sometimes it is a sign that you are following Jesus closely.

The key is to keep serving while the critics talk. Mandela did not win by arguing with every critic. He won by staying faithful to the mission for 27 years. Your mission may not take 27 years, but it will take staying power. Keep showing up. Keep loving people. Keep doing the next right thing.

This calls us to be better people because steady servants change the world around them. When you refuse to quit, the discouraged people watching you find hope. The young people watching you learn courage. The critics watching you sometimes even change their minds. Do not let "impossible" stop you. One faithful day at a time, the impossible gets done.

Prayer:

God, I am facing something that feels impossible, a broken relationship, a mountain of debt, a dream everyone says will never happen. When the world pushes back and people say it cannot be done, remind me that it always seems impossible until it is done. Give me stubborn faith and steady hands. I pray for everyone told their cause is hopeless today. Breathe courage into them. With You, no wall is too high, no night too long, and no battle too hard. Amen.
