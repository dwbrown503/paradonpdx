Week 25 · The Great Commission: Leading by Multiplying Servants

Thursday – Loved and Capable of Loving

"The greatest thing we can do is to help somebody know that they're loved and capable of loving." — Fred Rogers
Bible (ESV): John 13:34-35 — "A new commandment I give to you, that you love one another: just as I have loved you, you also are to love one another. By this all people will know that you are my disciples, if you have love for one another."

Fred Rogers, the kind man from the television neighborhood, understood something deep about Jesus. He said the greatest thing we can do is help somebody know two things: that they are loved, and that they are capable of loving. That is exactly what making disciples is about. First, we help people know they are loved by God. When someone truly knows they are loved, it changes everything inside them. Fear goes down. Hope comes up. They start to believe their life has meaning. Second, we help them know they are capable of loving too. That is the multiplying part. They do not just receive love. They start giving it. They become servants themselves. This is what Jesus meant when He said, "Love one another. As I have loved you, so you must love one another." He loved us first, and now we pass that love along. Notice that Jesus says the whole world will know we are His disciples by our love. Not by our perfect words. Not by our big buildings. By our love. So when you teach someone to serve, you are really teaching them they are loved and capable of loving. Maybe you invite a shy new person to help set up tea. You show them how, you thank them warmly, and you tell them what a difference their help made. In that small moment, they learn they matter. They learn they are loved. And soon they will be the one inviting someone else. That is how the neighborhood of God keeps growing, one loved and loving servant at a time.

Prayer:

Lord, the greatest thing I can do is help someone know they are loved and capable of loving. Today, show me who needs to hear it: the insecure teen, the discouraged spouse, the coworker who thinks they are a burden. Help me say it, show it, prove it with my time and attention. When I doubt my own lovability, remind me how You love me. I pray for everyone who feels unlovable tonight. Wrap them in love through someone like me. Amen.
