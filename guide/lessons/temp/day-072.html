Week 15 · Handling Criticism and Popularity

Tuesday – Worry Steals Today's Strength

"Worry does not empty tomorrow of its sorrow. It empties today of its strength." — Corrie ten Boom
Bible (ESV): Matthew 5:11-12 — "Blessed are you when others revile you and persecute you and utter all kinds of evil against you falsely on my account. Rejoice and be glad, for your reward is great in heaven, for so they persecuted the prophets who were before you."

Corrie ten Boom survived a Nazi prison camp, so she knew what real trouble looked like. She also knew that worry is a thief. It does not fix tomorrow. It only steals the strength you need for today. When leaders are criticized, worry whispers lies: What will they say next? What if everyone turns against me? What if I fail? Those thoughts drain your energy until you have nothing left to give the people who need you.

Jesus gives us a shocking answer to criticism in Matthew 5. He does not say, "Feel sorry for yourself when people insult you." He says, "Rejoice and be glad." That sounds crazy until you understand why. When you are criticized for doing what Jesus would do, you are in good company. The prophets faced the same thing. You are walking the same road as the heroes of faith.

This does not mean criticism never hurts. It hurts. But worry about criticism is optional, and it is poison. A servant leader who spends all day replaying mean words in their head has no strength left to serve. But a leader who hands the criticism to God and chooses joy stays strong enough to keep loving people, even the critics.

This calls us to be better people because a worry-free leader is a generous leader. When you are not drained by fear of what people think, you have energy to encourage your team, patience to listen to complaints without getting defensive, and kindness to spare for the very people who criticized you. Refuse to let worry steal today. Use today's strength to love today's people.

Prayer:

Lord, my mind keeps racing to tomorrow, what people will say, what might go wrong, whether I will be criticized. But worry only empties today of its strength. When others misunderstand me or speak against me for doing right, remind me that You called me blessed. Fill this day with Your strength instead of my fear. I pray for everyone lying awake with anxious thoughts tonight. Hold them close. Teach us to trust You with tomorrow so we can live fully today. Amen.
