Week 19 · Gethsemane: Submission to the Father's Will

Friday – Courage With Trembling Hands

"I learned that courage was not the absence of fear, but the triumph over it." — Nelson Mandela
Bible (ESV): John 14:27 — "Peace I leave with you; my peace I give to you. Not as the world gives do I give to you. Let not your hearts be troubled, neither let them be afraid."

Nelson Mandela spent 27 years in prison for standing against injustice. He knew fear well. He said the brave person is not the one who feels no fear, but the one who conquers it. Jesus proved this in Gethsemane. He said, "My soul is very sorrowful, even to death." His hands may well have trembled. But He still prayed, still surrendered, and still walked toward the cross. That is courage: trembling hands that keep holding on to God.

Many of us think courage means feeling confident. We wait to act until the fear goes away. But the fear may never go away, and waiting means we never move. Jesus shows us a better way. He felt the fear fully and obeyed anyway. His peace did not come from the absence of trouble. It came from the presence of the Father. That is the peace He promises us in John 14:27, a peace the world cannot give and the world cannot take away.

This calls us to be better people to those around us in a powerful way. When we are honest about our own fears, we give others permission to be honest about theirs. A leader who says, "I was scared too, but God carried me," is far more helpful than one who pretends to be fearless. Our courage can become someone else's comfort.

So do not wait until you feel brave. Take the trembling hands you have, lift them to God in prayer, and take the next step. Courage is not the absence of fear. It is fear that has been handed to God, and hands that keep serving anyway.

Prayer:

God, I am afraid, and my hands tremble at what I must do: the apology I owe, the truth I must tell, the stand I must take. But courage is not the absence of fear; it is going forward anyway. Fill my trembling hands with Your peace, the peace Jesus promised, not the world's. Steady me. I pray for everyone facing something frightening today. Turn their fear into triumph. Let me do the scary right thing, trembling and all, trusting You hold me. Amen.
