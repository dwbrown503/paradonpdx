Week 21 · The Crucifixion: Forgiving from the Cross

Wednesday – Strong Enough to Forgive

"The weak can never forgive. Forgiveness is the attribute of the strong." — Mahatma Gandhi
Bible (ESV): Matthew 6:14-15 — "For if you forgive others their trespasses, your heavenly Father will also forgive you, but if you do not forgive others their trespasses, neither will your Father forgive your trespasses."

Many people think forgiveness is weakness. They think it means letting someone walk all over you, or saying what they did was okay. Gandhi saw the opposite truth: only the strong can forgive. It takes no strength to hold a grudge. Anyone can stay angry. But it takes real strength to look at someone who hurt you deeply and choose to release them.

Jesus showed this strength on the cross. It would have been easy for Him to curse His killers. He had all power. Instead, He used His strength to forgive. That is what makes the cross so powerful. It was not weakness that kept Jesus on that cross. It was strength, love strong enough to forgive the very people driving the nails.

Jesus also taught something serious in Matthew 6:14-15. Our forgiveness of others is tied to God's forgiveness of us. This does not mean we earn God's forgiveness by forgiving others. It means a heart that has truly received God's forgiveness will naturally overflow with forgiveness toward others. If we refuse to forgive, it may show we have not really understood how much we have been forgiven.

What does strong forgiveness look like in daily life? It looks like a wife forgiving a husband who spoke harshly, not because it did not hurt, but because love is stronger than pride. It looks like a worker forgiving a boss who was unfair, and still doing excellent work. It looks like forgiving a parent for old wounds, and choosing to build something new. This kind of strength does not come from us. It comes from God. Ask Him for it, and He will give it.

Prayer:

God, they say the weak can never forgive, that forgiveness belongs to the strong. Today, make me strong. Give me the strength to forgive my spouse's harsh words, my friend's betrayal, and my own repeated failures. When I forgive others, You forgive me. Let that promise make me brave. I pray for everyone who thinks they are too weak to forgive. Strengthen them. Prove in my life today that forgiveness is not for the soft, but for the strong. Amen.
