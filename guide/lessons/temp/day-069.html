Week 14 · Prayer and Solitude as Fuel for Service

Thursday – One Step of Faith

"Faith is taking the first step even when you don't see the whole staircase." — Martin Luther King Jr.
Bible (ESV): Matthew 6:6 — "But when you pray, go into your room and shut the door and pray to your Father who is in secret. And your Father who sees in secret will reward you."

Dr. King led millions of people through some of the darkest days in American history, and he did not have all the answers. He could not see the whole staircase. He could only see the next step. That is what faith looks like, and it is also what prayer looks like.

When you go into your room, shut the door, and pray, you cannot see God with your eyes. You cannot see how He will answer. You are taking a step in the dark, trusting that your Father is there in the secret place, listening. That takes faith. But Jesus promises that the Father who sees in secret will reward you. He does not say how or when. He just says trust Me and take the step.

Servant leaders face this every day. You step up to help someone and you do not know if it will work. You start a new ministry and you cannot see the ending. You forgive someone and you do not know if they will change. Prayer in the secret place is where you get the courage to take those steps anyway.

This calls us to be better people because faith-filled people are brave for others. When you trust God in the quiet, you become bold in the open. You visit the sick even when it is uncomfortable. You stand up for someone being treated badly even when it costs you. You give even when your own wallet feels thin. Your secret faith becomes public love, and the people around you learn to trust God too, just by watching you take that first step.

Prayer:

Father, today I face something that scares me, a hard conversation, a big decision, a step I cannot see the end of. Like Jesus taught, I come to You in the quiet of my room first. Give me the faith to take just one step, even when I cannot see the whole staircase. When my knees shake and my voice trembles, hold me steady. I pray for everyone standing at the edge of a frightening step today. Walk with us. Remind us that we never step alone when we step with You. Amen.
