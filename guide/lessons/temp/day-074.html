Week 15 · Handling Criticism and Popularity

Thursday – Feed Just One

"If you can't feed a hundred people, then feed just one." — Mother Teresa
Bible (ESV): Proverbs 29:25 — "The fear of man lays a snare, but whoever trusts in the Lord is safe."

Mother Teresa worked in the slums of Calcutta, India, where the need was so big it could crush your heart. Millions of poor people. Sickness everywhere. Critics said her work was too small to matter. Why bother? You cannot fix all of this. But she answered with this simple truth: if you cannot feed a hundred, feed just one. She refused to let the size of the problem, or the voices of the critics, stop her from helping the one person right in front of her.

This is a powerful answer to both criticism and popularity. Some leaders only act when the crowd is big enough to notice. They want the hundred, the thousand, the viral moment. And when critics say the work is too small, they quit. But Mother Teresa teaches us a better way. Serve the one. Love the one. The size of the audience does not decide the size of the love.

Proverbs warns us that fear of man is a snare, a trap. When we fear what people think, we get trapped. We only serve when it looks impressive. We only speak when it is safe. But when we trust in the Lord, we are kept safe. Safe to do small, faithful work that no one sees. Safe to keep going when the critics laugh at how little we are doing.

This calls us to be better people because the world is changed one person at a time. The tired coworker you encourage. The lonely neighbor you visit. The child you listen to. These small acts may never make the news, but they make heaven rejoice. Do not wait until you can feed a hundred. Feed just one, today, with all your heart.

Prayer:

Lord, I am afraid of what people think, afraid to look foolish, afraid to fail in front of others. But the fear of people is a trap, and I want to be free. Today, help me trust You more than I fear their opinions. Show me just one person to feed, help, or encourage, one small act, done bravely. I pray for everyone paralyzed by people's judgment today. Set us free. Let our quiet faithfulness matter more than anyone's applause. Amen.
