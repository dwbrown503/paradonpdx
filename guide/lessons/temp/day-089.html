Week 18 · The Upper Room and the Basin (John 13)

Thursday – Losing Yourself to Find Yourself

"The best way to find yourself is to lose yourself in the service of others." — Mahatma Gandhi
Bible (ESV): Matthew 20:26-28 — "It shall not be so among you. But whoever would be great among you must be your servant, and whoever would be first among you must be your slave, even as the Son of Man came not to be served but to serve, and to give his life as a ransom for many."

Many people spend their whole lives asking, "Who am I? What is my purpose?" They look for the answer in success, in money, in popularity, or in doing whatever makes them happy. But Gandhi pointed to a surprising answer: you find yourself by losing yourself in serving others. Jesus said the same thing long before Gandhi. Whoever would be great must be a servant.

Why does this work? Because we were made in the image of a serving God. Jesus said He came not to be served but to serve. When we serve, we are living the way we were designed to live. It is like a fish finally getting into water. Suddenly everything makes sense. The emptiness fades because we are doing what we were made for.

Think of a time you helped someone and walked away feeling more alive than when you started. Maybe you volunteered at a food bank, or helped a friend move, or spent time with an elderly person who was lonely. You gave your time and energy, but you received something back: purpose, joy, and a quiet knowing that your life matters.

This calls us to be better people because it shifts our focus outward. Instead of asking, "What can I get today?" we ask, "Who can I help today?" That one question changes everything. It changes how we treat our families, our coworkers, and even strangers. A life poured out for others is never a wasted life. It is the fullest life there is.

Prayer:

Lord, I hold my plans so tightly, my schedule, my comfort, my way. Today, teach me to loosen my grip. When someone needs me and it ruins my plans, help me choose them with joy. Show me the strange truth Jesus taught: that losing my life is how I find it. I pray for everyone clinging to control today. Free us. Let me discover the deep happiness that comes from giving myself away, at home, at work, wherever You send me. Amen.
