Week 21 · The Crucifixion: Forgiving from the Cross

Monday – Forgive Anyway

"Forgiveness is an act of the will, and the will can function regardless of the temperature of the heart." — Corrie ten Boom
Bible (ESV): Luke 23:34 — "And Jesus said, 'Father, forgive them, for they know not what they do.' And they cast lots to divide his garments."

Corrie ten Boom knew about forgiveness. She survived a Nazi concentration camp where her sister died. After the war, she met one of her former guards face to face, and he asked her to forgive him. Her heart felt cold. Every feeling in her screamed no. But she chose to extend her hand anyway. She later wrote that forgiveness is an act of the will, and the will can work even when the heart feels cold.

Jesus proved this on the cross. Nailed to wood, bleeding, mocked by the crowd, He prayed, "Father, forgive them, for they know not what they do." He did not feel warm toward the soldiers. He chose to forgive them. Forgiveness was not a feeling for Jesus. It was a decision.

This changes everything about how we forgive. Most of us wait until we feel like forgiving. We wait for the anger to fade, for the apology to come, for our hearts to warm up. But feelings are slow, and sometimes they never come. Jesus shows us we do not have to wait. We can choose to forgive as an act of the will, trusting God to warm our hearts later.

What does this look like in real life? It looks like saying, "I forgive you," even when your voice shakes. It looks like praying for someone who hurt you even when you do not feel like it. It looks like refusing to bring up the old hurt in the next argument. You may not feel the forgiveness fully yet, and that is okay. The will can lead, and the heart will follow. That is how we become better people: not by waiting for perfect feelings, but by making brave choices like Jesus did.

Prayer:

Father, they do not deserve my forgiveness, and that is exactly the point. Jesus forgave from the cross while they were still driving the nails. Today, help me forgive anyway: the debt unpaid, the promise broken, the wound still fresh. My will can choose what my heart cannot yet feel. I pray for everyone waiting for an apology that will never come. Free them to forgive first. Set my heart free as I set them free. Teach me that forgiveness is my choice, not their earning. Amen.
