Week 25 · The Great Commission: Leading by Multiplying Servants

Friday – Little Bits of Good That Overwhelm the World

"Do your little bit of good where you are; it's those little bits of good put together that overwhelm the world." — Desmond Tutu
Bible (ESV): John 14:12 — "Truly, truly, I say to you, whoever believes in me will also do the works that I do; and greater works than these will he do, because I am going to the Father."

Desmond Tutu gave us a hopeful promise. You do not have to fix the whole world by yourself. You only have to do your little bit of good where you are. Then someone else does their little bit of good where they are. And when all those little bits come together, they overwhelm the world. That is the whole secret of the Great Commission. Jesus did not ask twelve men to save the planet alone. He taught them, and they taught others, and those others taught still more. Every little bit of good added together became a flood of love that is still moving today. And Jesus made an amazing promise in John 14:12. He said that whoever believes in Him will do the works He did, and even greater things. Greater things! How could anyone do greater things than Jesus? The answer is multiplication. One Jesus on earth could touch thousands. But millions of servants, all taught by one another across the centuries, can touch billions. That is you. That is us. Your little bit of good matters more than you know. When you welcome someone at the door with a real smile, when you pour a cup of coffee with care, when you set out communion with gentle hands, when you teach a new volunteer to do the same, you are adding your little bit to the great flood. Do not wait until you feel big enough. Do not wait until everything is perfect. Do your little bit of good today, right where you are, and teach someone else to do theirs. Together, servant by servant, we will overwhelm the world with the love of Jesus.

Prayer:

Father, You promised that whoever believes will do even greater works, and it starts with little bits of good. Today, I will do mine where I am: the kind email, the extra tip, the prayer for a stranger, the trash picked up. When these acts feel too small to matter, remind me they join millions of others to overwhelm the world. I pray for every small doer of good today. Multiply us. Let my little bit be part of Your big victory. Amen.
