Tuesday – How Much Love We Put In

"Love begins at home, and it is not how much we do... but how much love we put in that action." — Mother Teresa
Bible (ESV): John 15:13 — "Greater love has no one than this, that someone lay down his life for his friends."

Jesus said the greatest love is laying down your life for your friends. He did exactly that on the cross. But Mother Teresa reminds us that laying down our lives does not always mean dying. Most days, it means putting love into the small, ordinary actions right in front of us, starting at home. It is not how much we do. It is how much love we put into what we do.

Two people can do the exact same task, and one act can be full of love while the other is empty. One person washes dishes with grumbling and resentment. Another washes the same dishes thinking, "I love my family, and I want them to come home to a clean kitchen." Same dishes, completely different acts. The difference is the love put into it.

This changes how we see our daily work. The parent making lunches, the worker doing a repetitive job, the volunteer setting up chairs, the friend listening to the same story for the third time — all of these can be acts of great love if love is put into them. Jesus took the lowest job, washing feet, and made it holy by the love He put into it.

How does this call us to be better people? It teaches us that no task is too small to matter. We stop rushing through our duties and start filling them with love. We stop counting how much we do and start asking how much love we are putting in. A home, a workplace, or a church filled with people who put love into everything they do becomes a little picture of heaven. And it all starts with the example Jesus set when He laid down His life for His friends.

Prayer:

God, it is not how much I do, but how much love I put into it. Today, at home where no one applauds, help me love the ordinary: the laundry folded with care, the dinner cooked with joy, the floors swept with a glad heart, the listening ear for my tired spouse. When housework feels invisible, remind me heaven sees. I pray for every weary homemaker and parent tonight. Bless their hidden love. Let my home be the first place my love shows up. Amen.
