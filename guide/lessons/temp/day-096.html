Week 20 · The Trials: Silence Under False Accusation

Monday – No Payback

"An eye for an eye only ends up making the whole world blind." — Mahatma Gandhi
Bible (ESV): 1 Peter 2:23 — "When he was reviled, he did not revile in return; when he suffered, he did not threaten, but continued entrusting himself to him who judges justly."

During His trials, Jesus was lied about, insulted, spit on, and beaten. False witnesses twisted His words. The religious leaders who should have recognized Him treated Him like a criminal. He had every right to fight back. With one word He could have called down angels. Instead, He did nothing to get even. When He was reviled, He did not revile in return. When He suffered, He did not threaten. He entrusted Himself to God.

Gandhi saw the same truth: if everyone pays back hurt with hurt, the whole world ends up blind. Revenge feels good for a moment, but it never heals anything. It just spreads the pain around. Jesus broke the cycle. He absorbed the hate and answered with silence and trust.

This is one of the hardest lessons in the Christian life. When someone lies about you, gossips about you, or treats you unfairly, everything inside you wants payback. You want to defend yourself, set the record straight, or make them feel what you felt. Jesus shows us another way: entrust yourself to the One who judges justly. That does not mean we never tell the truth or never seek justice. It means we do not take revenge into our own hands. We leave the final judgment to God.

How does this make us better people? It frees us. The person who must have payback is a prisoner of the person who hurt them. The person who entrusts the hurt to God is free. And it shows the world something beautiful: a person who has been wronged yet refuses to become bitter looks exactly like Jesus.

Prayer:

Lord Jesus, when they insulted You, You did not insult back; when You suffered, You did not threaten. Today, when the coworker mocks me, the driver cuts me off, or the family member twists my words, help me choose no payback. Swallow my sharp reply before it leaves my mouth. Trust You to make things right. I pray for everyone nursing revenge fantasies tonight. Release them. Teach me that refusing to retaliate is not weakness. It is walking in Your footsteps. Amen.
