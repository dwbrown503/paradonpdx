Week 8 · Seeking the Lost vs. Seeking Status

Thursday – Leaving the Comfortable to Find the One

"Worry does not empty tomorrow of its sorrow, it empties today of its strength." – Corrie ten Boom
Bible (ESV): Luke 15:5-6 – "And when he has found it, he lays it on his shoulders, rejoicing. And when he comes home, he calls together his friends and his neighbors, saying to them, ‘Rejoice with me, for I have found my sheep that was lost.’"

Think about what the shepherd had to do. He left ninety-nine safe sheep in the open country. That was risky. He walked into the hills, maybe in the dark, maybe in danger, to find one lost sheep. He did not sit at home worrying about what might happen tomorrow. He used today's strength to go and search. And when he found the sheep, he did not scold it. He joyfully put it on his shoulders and carried it home. Then he threw a party.

Corrie ten Boom said that worry empties today of its strength. Many of us want to help lost people, but we worry too much. We worry about what to say. We worry about being rejected. We worry about leaving our comfort zone. So we do nothing, and today passes by unused. A servant leader does not let worry steal today's strength. They get up and go. They call the friend who stopped answering. They visit the neighbor who is struggling. They invite the lonely person to dinner. It might feel risky or awkward, but love is stronger than worry. And when that lost person comes home, there is joy in heaven and joy in your heart. Do not spend today worrying. Spend it searching.

Prayer:

Dear God, I confess that I worry too much and act too little. I stay comfortable on my couch while lost people wander alone. Forgive me. Today, take my worry and turn it into action. Help me pick up the phone, knock on the door, invite the lonely neighbor for coffee. I pray for everyone who is lost today, far from home and far from You. Give me courage to leave my comfort and go find them. And when the lost are found, let my heart celebrate with all of heaven. Amen.
