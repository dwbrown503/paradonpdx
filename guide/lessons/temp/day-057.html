Tuesday – Giving With a Happy Heart

"Do your little bit of good where you are; it's those little bits of good put together that overwhelm the world." — Desmond Tutu
Bible (ESV): 2 Corinthians 9:7 — "Each one must give as he has decided in his heart, not reluctantly or under compulsion, for God loves a cheerful giver."

Nobody likes being forced to share. When someone makes you give, it does not feel good. It feels like a rule, not like love. God does not want that kind of giving. He wants our hearts to be happy when we give. He loves a cheerful giver.

Think about it this way. If a friend shares their lunch with you because they have to, you can feel it. It feels cold. But if a friend shares because they are glad to, the meal tastes better. The same is true when we help people in need. A happy heart makes the gift bigger than what is in the box or bag.

Maybe you are dropping off food at a food pantry. Maybe you are buying a hot meal for someone standing on a corner. Maybe you are cooking for a neighbor who is sick. If you do it with a grumpy heart, the gift loses its warmth. But if you do it with joy, people feel loved, not just fed.

God is not counting your cans or your dollars. He is looking at your heart. Decide in your heart what to give, and give it gladly. When many people each do their little bit of good with happy hearts, those little bits come together and change the whole world around them. So the next time you have a chance to share, do not sigh and do it anyway. Smile and do it with joy. Your happy heart is part of the gift, and it may be the part people remember the most.

Prayer:

Father, thank You for the joy of giving. Today, free me from the grumbling that turns generosity into duty. When I tip the server, drop money in the offering, or buy groceries for a struggling family, let me do it with a happy heart, not reluctantly, not to be seen, but because I love You. Remind me that everything I have came from Your hand. Make my giving cheerful, my sharing quick, and my heart light. Bless every cheerful giver today with the deep joy only You can give. Amen.
