Week 15 · Handling Criticism and Popularity

Monday – The Time Is Always Right

"The time is always right to do what is right." — Martin Luther King Jr.
Bible (ESV): Galatians 1:10 — "For am I now seeking the approval of man, or of God? Or am I trying to please man? If I were still trying to please man, I would not be a servant of Christ."

Dr. King said these words while facing some of the harshest criticism any leader has ever faced. People called him names. People threatened his life. Powerful people told him to wait, to slow down, to stop. But he kept doing what was right, because he answered to God first, not to the crowd.

Every servant leader will face this same test. When you stand up for what is right, some people will cheer and some people will criticize. If you make your choices based on who is clapping, you will be tossed around like a leaf in the wind. One day you do this to please one group, the next day you change to please another. That is no way to lead.

Paul gives us the cure in Galatians. He asks a straight question: am I trying to please people, or God? A servant of Christ answers to God. That does not mean we ignore people or stop caring about their feelings. It means God's approval is the anchor that holds us steady when the crowd's opinion shifts.

This calls us to be better people because doing right even when it is unpopular is one of the bravest forms of love. It means telling the truth kindly when a lie would be easier. It means including the left-out person when the popular thing is to ignore them. It means staying honest when cheating would win applause. When you do what is right no matter who is watching, the people around you learn that integrity is real, and they start to live it too.

Prayer:

God, give me the courage to do what is right even when it is unpopular. Today at work or at school, when everyone else is cutting corners or laughing at someone's expense, help me stand for what pleases You, not people. Free me from the need for applause. The time is always right to do right, in the morning meeting, at the lunch table, in the group chat. I pray for everyone pressured to go along with wrong today. Strengthen them. Let my life seek Your approval above all. Amen.
