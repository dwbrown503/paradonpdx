Week 22 · The Ransom Paid: Death as Ultimate Service

Thursday – Report for Duty

"Don't bother to give God instructions; just report for duty." — Corrie ten Boom
Bible (ESV): Philippians 2:8 — "And being found in human form, he humbled himself by becoming obedient to the point of death, even death on a cross."

Corrie ten Boom learned this lesson the hard way. She had plans for her life, and God had different plans that included a concentration camp. She discovered that the best prayer is not telling God what to do, but simply showing up and saying, "I am here. Use me." Just report for duty.

Jesus is the perfect picture of this. The verse says He humbled Himself by becoming obedient to the point of death, even death on a cross. Jesus did not give the Father instructions. He did not negotiate the terms. He did not ask for an easier assignment. He simply reported for duty and obeyed, all the way to the cross. His obedience was not partial or conditional. It was complete.

Most of us do the opposite. We come to God with a list of instructions: "God, here is what I need You to do, and here is how I need You to do it." We treat God like our assistant instead of our Commander. Then we wonder why our prayers feel unanswered. Corrie's wisdom turns it around. Our job is not to instruct God. Our job is to show up, ready to obey.

What does reporting for duty look like in real life? It looks like starting the day with, "Lord, what do You have for me today?" instead of, "Lord, here is my plan, please bless it." It looks like serving where you are needed, not just where you prefer. It looks like saying yes to the hard assignment, the awkward conversation, or the unseen task, because God asked. This makes us better people because it kills our demand to control everything. We become servants instead of managers, soldiers instead of generals, and we discover the deep peace of simply obeying a Father we trust.

Prayer:

God, I will not give You instructions today. I simply report for duty. Show me my assignment: the task at work, the chore at home, the errand run with kindness, the person who needs me. Like Jesus, obedient even to death, help me obey in the small things with a glad heart. When duty feels dull, remind me who I serve. I pray for everyone dragging through their duties today. Renew their strength. Let my daily obedience be my worship, offered with joy. Amen.
