Week 23 · The Resurrection: Vindicated Humility

Friday – Rise Again

"The greatest glory in living lies not in never falling, but in rising every time we fall." — Nelson Mandela
Bible (ESV): 1 Peter 5:6 — "Humble yourselves, therefore, under the mighty hand of God so that at the proper time he may exalt you."

Peter loved Jesus, but on the night Jesus was arrested, Peter was scared. Three times people asked him if he knew Jesus, and three times he said no. Then the rooster crowed, and Peter went outside and cried. He had failed his best friend. He probably thought his story was over.

But it was not over. After Jesus rose from the dead, He found Peter. On the beach, Jesus asked Peter three times, "Do you love me?" Three times Peter said yes. And each time, Jesus gave him a job: "Feed my lambs." "Tend my sheep." "Feed my sheep." Jesus did not throw Peter away. He lifted him back up and trusted him again. Peter went on to become a great leader in the early church.

Nelson Mandela said the greatest glory in life is not never falling, but rising every time we fall. We all fall. We say the wrong thing. We hurt people we love. We make choices we regret. Humility is what helps us rise again. A proud person hides the fall and pretends it never happened. A humble person says, "I was wrong. God, help me get up." And God does.

The resurrection is God's great promise that falling is never the end. Jesus went all the way down into death, and God raised Him up. Whatever pit you are in today, it is not too deep for Him. Humble yourself under His mighty hand. Tell Him the truth about where you are. Then take His hand and stand up. He will lift you in His perfect time, and He will give you a new beginning — just like He did for Peter.

Prayer:

God, I have fallen, the diet broken, the temper lost, the promise I failed to keep. But Your greatest glory is not in never falling; it is in rising every time. Today, help me rise again. Wash me clean, set my feet straight, and let me start fresh without shame. I pray for everyone who fell today and wants to stay down. Lift them. I humble myself under Your mighty hand, and in Your time, lift me up to try again. Amen.
