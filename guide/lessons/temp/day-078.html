Wednesday – Your Little Bit of Good

"Do your little bit of good where you are; it's those little bits of good put together that overwhelm the world." — Desmond Tutu
Bible (ESV): Micah 6:8 — "He has told you, O man, what is good; and what does the Lord require of you but to do justice, and to love kindness, and to walk humbly with your God?"

Desmond Tutu helped lead South Africa out of apartheid, one of the greatest evils of the last hundred years. He did not do it alone, and he did not do it with one giant act. He did it with millions of little bits of good, done by ordinary people, put together until they overwhelmed the darkness. Your little bit matters more than you think.

Micah 6:8 gives us the simple recipe for those little bits: act justly, love mercy, and walk humbly with God. Notice how small and daily those words are. Act justly means do the fair thing in today's decisions. Love mercy means be kind to someone who does not deserve it today. Walk humbly means remember you are not the hero of the story, God is. None of these require a stage or a spotlight. They just require you, where you are, today.

Servant leaders sometimes dream so big they miss the small. They want to change the whole city while ignoring the lonely person at their own table. But Tutu's wisdom flips that around. Change the table first. Be fair in the small meeting. Show mercy to the difficult volunteer. Walk humbly when you are praised. Those little bits add up, and when enough servants do their little bit, the world gets overwhelmed with good.

This calls us to be better people because you do not have to wait until you are powerful to be good. You can start right where you are, with what you have, today. Smile at the tired cashier. Share your lunch. Tell the truth when a lie would be easier. Your little bit of good, joined with everyone else's, is how God overwhelms the darkness.

Prayer:

God, thank You that my little bit of good matters. Today, I will do it where I am: hold the door for the stranger, write the thank-you note, pick up the litter on my street, smile at the lonely kid. These small things feel tiny, but You say they overwhelm the world when we all do them. Forgive me for waiting to do something big. I pray for every quiet doer of good today. Bless their small acts. Let my little bit join theirs, and watch what You do with it. Amen.
