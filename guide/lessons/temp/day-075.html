Week 15 · Handling Criticism and Popularity

Friday – Lose Yourself in Service

"The best way to find yourself is to lose yourself in the service of others." — Mahatma Gandhi
Bible (ESV): Mark 10:45 — "For even the Son of Man came not to be served but to serve, and to give his life as a ransom for many."

Gandhi noticed something strange about human hearts. The more we chase after ourselves, our image, our fame, our comfort, the more lost we feel. But the moment we forget about ourselves and start serving others, we find out who we really are. It is a beautiful paradox: you find yourself by losing yourself.

Jesus lived this perfectly. He said He came not to be served but to serve, and to give His life as a ransom for many. He was the King of everything, yet He washed dirty feet. He deserved all the praise in the world, yet He took all the criticism without fighting back. He lost Himself completely in serving us, and in doing so He showed us exactly who He is: love itself.

This is the final answer to both criticism and popularity. The leader who is chasing popularity will always be shaken by criticism, because their identity is built on what people think. But the leader who has lost themselves in service is unshakable, because their identity is built on love. Critics can take away your reputation, but they cannot take away your love. Crowds can stop clapping, but they cannot stop you from serving.

This calls us to be better people because self-forgetful servants are the happiest people on earth. They are not keeping score. They are not checking who noticed. They are simply giving, and giving fills the heart in a way that getting never can. This week, try losing yourself. Serve someone who cannot pay you back. Love someone who may never thank you. You will find yourself, and you will find joy.

Prayer:

God, I am tired of thinking about myself, my wants, my image, my plans. Today, help me lose myself in serving others. Show me the tired mom who needs a break, the elderly neighbor who needs groceries, the coworker drowning in work. Let me pour my energy into them and forget to keep score. Like Jesus, who came not to be served but to serve, teach me that the best way to find my life is to give it away. Fill my self-forgetfulness with Your joy. Amen.
