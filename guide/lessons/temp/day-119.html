Week 24 · Restoring the Fallen Leader (Peter on the Beach)

Thursday – Follow Me, Even When It's Hard

"I learned that courage was not the absence of fear, but the triumph over it. The brave man is not he who does not feel afraid, but he who conquers that fear." — Nelson Mandela
Bible (ESV): John 21:18-19 — "Truly, truly, I say to you, when you were young, you used to dress yourself and walk wherever you wanted, but when you are old, you will stretch out your hands, and another will dress you and carry you where you do not want to go. (This he said to show by what kind of death he was to glorify God.) And after saying this he said to him, ‘Follow me.’"

Right after restoring Peter, Jesus told him something hard. He said that one day Peter would be taken where he did not want to go — that he would die for his faith. Jesus did not sugarcoat it. Restoration did not come with a promise of an easy life. It came with two words: "Follow me!"

Think about what that took. Peter had just fallen apart under pressure. He knew how fear felt. And now Jesus was telling him, "The road ahead will be harder, not easier." Yet Peter said yes. He followed. And history tells us he did — Peter became a bold, fearless leader of the early church, and he never denied Jesus again.

Nelson Mandela knew about this kind of courage. He spent 27 years in prison for standing up for what was right, and he said that courage is not the absence of fear. Courage is feeling afraid and going forward anyway. Peter was afraid on the night he denied Jesus. But after the beach, he learned that fear does not get the final say.

This is a lesson for all of us. Following Jesus does not mean life gets easy. It means we get a Leader worth following through hard things. When we fail, the temptation is to quit — to decide that serving is too risky, that we will just play it safe. But restoration says the opposite: get back up, feel the fear, and follow anyway. A servant leader is not someone who never feels afraid. A servant leader is someone who follows Jesus even with shaking hands. Peter proves it can be done. If the man who denied Jesus three times could become the rock of the church, then your failure does not disqualify you from a brave future either.

Prayer:

God, following You will not always be easy. Jesus told Peter it would cost him everything. Today, when following means the hard road, telling the truth when lying is easier, staying when leaving is tempting, giving when keeping feels safer, help me follow anyway. When I want the comfortable path, remind me who walks beside me. I pray for everyone paying a price for following You today. Walk with them. Let my "yes" hold firm, even when it is hard. Amen.
