Thursday – Sharing Like Family

"The purpose of human life is to serve, and to show compassion and the will to help others." — Albert Schweitzer
Bible (ESV): Acts 2:44-45 — "And all who believed were together and had all things in common. And they were selling their possessions and belongings and distributing the proceeds to all, as any had need."

The first Christians lived in a special way. They were together, and they shared everything they had. If someone needed food, the others made sure that person was fed. If someone needed help, the others sold some of their own things to help. They treated each other like family — because that is what they were.

Think about how a good family works. When one person is hungry, the family does not say, "That is your problem." They set another plate at the table. When one person is struggling, the others step in. That is what these early believers did. Nobody went without, because everyone shared.

This is a picture of what the church is meant to be. Not a group of people who only look out for themselves, but a family that takes care of each other and of the neighbors around them. When we share our food, our money, and our time with people in need, we are living like that early church. We are saying, "You belong. You are part of this family."

It does not mean we all have to sell everything we own. It means our hearts should be open. When we see a need, we respond. When someone is hungry, we feed them. When someone is hurting, we help. That is the purpose of our lives: to serve and to show compassion, like one big family under God. And a family does not keep score. They just keep loving. The next time you share a meal or lend a hand, do it with the warmth of family. That is what makes the church feel like home to people who have never had one.

Prayer:

Father, thank You for the early church, who shared everything like one big family. Today, loosen my grip on "mine." Help me lend the tool, share the meal, offer the ride, open my home, without keeping score. Forgive me for the times I held back what someone else needed. Make my street, my workplace, and my church feel like family, where nobody goes without. Teach me that my things are really Your things, entrusted to me for others. Let my sharing show the world what Your family looks like. Amen.
