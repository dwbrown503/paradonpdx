Week 9 · Touching the Untouchable (Leper Cleansing)

Tuesday – Putting Down Our Stones

"If you judge people, you have no time to love them." — Mother Teresa
Bible (ESV): Luke 6:37 — "Judge not, and you will not be judged; condemn not, and you will not be condemned; forgive, and you will be forgiven;"

Long before the leper met Jesus, the crowd had already judged him. They had decided he was unclean, dangerous, and not worth their time. Nobody asked how he felt. Nobody asked his story. The label said it all. We do the same thing today, maybe without even thinking about it. We see a man sleeping on a park bench and decide he is lazy. We see a young mom with three kids in the checkout line using food assistance and decide she is making bad choices. We see a coworker who never comes to the church potluck and decide he does not care about God. Every one of those quick judgments builds a wall. It keeps us from loving the real person behind the label. Jesus never picked up a stone. When the man with leprosy came near, Jesus did not ask what he had done wrong or whether he deserved healing. He saw a person in pain and he acted with love. Putting down our stones means we stop writing people off. It means we choose curiosity over judgment. Instead of asking "What is wrong with that person?", we can ask "I wonder what that person has been through." A servant leader does not have to agree with every choice someone has made. But a servant leader refuses to let a label be the last word. When we forgive quickly and judge slowly, we make room for love to do its work. And love is what changes hearts, not judgment.

Prayer:

Heavenly Father, I am sorry for the times I judged people before I ever knew them, the labels I placed, the gossip I repeated, the walls I built with my thoughts. Forgive me for picking up stones instead of lending a hand. Today, when I meet someone with a rough past or a different lifestyle, help me see them as loved and valuable, the way You see them. Teach me to be slow to judge and quick to forgive. Fill my heart with Your love so no room is left for condemning others. Amen.
