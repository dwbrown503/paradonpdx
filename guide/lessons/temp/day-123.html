Week 25 · The Great Commission: Leading by Multiplying Servants

Wednesday – The Shepherd Leads from Behind

"A leader... is like a shepherd. He stays behind the flock, letting the most nimble go out ahead, whereupon the others follow, not realizing that all along they are being directed from behind." — Nelson Mandela
Bible (ESV): 2 Timothy 2:2 — "and what you have heard from me in the presence of many witnesses entrust to faithful men, who will be able to teach others also."

Nelson Mandela gave us a wonderful picture of a leader. He said a good leader is like a shepherd. The shepherd does not always walk in front shouting orders. Sometimes the shepherd stays behind the flock, gently guiding, while the sheep walk ahead and grow confident. That is what multiplying servants looks like. You do not always have to be the one up front. A wise leader raises others up and then steps back so they can shine. The Apostle Paul did this with Timothy. Paul taught Timothy everything he could. Then he told Timothy to find reliable people and teach them too, so those people could teach others. That is four generations of servants: Paul taught Timothy, Timothy taught reliable people, and those people taught others. That is how the love of Jesus kept spreading long after Paul was gone. In real life, this means you teach someone to do your job well and then you let them do it. You stand behind them, ready to help if they need you, but you let them take the lead. Maybe you have greeted people at the door for years. Now you train a new greeter, stand beside them once or twice, and then step back with a smile. It can feel hard to let go. We like being needed. But a shepherd knows the flock is strongest when the sheep learn to walk. When you lead from behind, you are not losing your place. You are making more room in the Kingdom for others to find theirs.

Prayer:

God, teach me to lead like a shepherd, staying behind the flock, letting others go ahead, guiding gently. Today, when I lead the meeting, the team, or my family, help me push others forward instead of pulling rank. Celebrate their wins louder than my own. Entrust what I have learned to faithful people who will teach others too. I pray for every leader tempted to control everything. Loosen our grip. Show me the quiet strength of leading from behind. Amen.
