Week 13 · Rebuking Religious Exploitation (Cleansing the Temple)

Tuesday – A House of Prayer

"This is what the past is for! Every experience God gives us, every person He puts in our lives is the perfect preparation for the future that only He can see." — Corrie ten Boom
Bible (ESV): Matthew 21:13 — "He said to them, 'It is written, "My house shall be called a house of prayer," but you make it a den of robbers.'"

Jesus walked into the temple and called it what God intended it to be: a house of prayer. A house of prayer is a place where anyone can come close to God. A place of peace, welcome, and honesty. But the people in charge had turned it into something else — "a den of robbers." Instead of helping people pray, they were robbing them. They charged high prices for the animals people needed for worship. The poor paid the most. God's house had become a place of profit instead of a place of prayer.

Notice what Jesus cares about. He does not say the temple should be a house of rules, or a house of shows, or a house of business. He says it should be a house of prayer. Prayer means talking to God and listening to Him. It is simple and honest. When prayer is at the center, everything else stays honest too. But when money and power take the center, people get hurt.

What does this mean for our churches and our lives today? A church is not a business. It is not a club for the important. It is God's house — open to everyone. When someone new walks through the door, do they feel welcome? Or do they feel like an outsider? When a poor family sits in the back row, do they feel loved, or do they feel judged?

Corrie ten Boom said that every experience God gives us, and every person He puts in our lives, is preparation for the future only He can see. Maybe God has let you feel left out before, so that today you can welcome someone who feels left out. Maybe you have been hurt by someone who used religion for power, so that today you can show people what real, gentle faith looks like.

Ask yourself: is my heart a house of prayer, or have I let other things take over? Keep prayer at the center. Welcome everyone. That is what God's house is for.

Prayer:

Father, You said Your house shall be called a house of prayer. Today, make my home a house of prayer, a place where people find peace, not pressure; welcome, not sales pitches. Forgive me for the noise and busyness I let fill my rooms. Teach me to pray with my family, to bless the people who walk through my door, to keep one quiet corner for You. I pray for our churches. Keep them houses of prayer for all people, never marketplaces. Let everyone who enters find You waiting with open arms. Amen.
