Week 14 · Prayer and Solitude as Fuel for Service

Friday – Listening Is the First Service

"The first service that one owes to others in the fellowship consists in listening to them." — Dietrich Bonhoeffer
Bible (ESV): Psalm 46:10 — "Be still, and know that I am God. I will be exalted among the nations, I will be exalted in the earth!"

Bonhoeffer was a pastor in Germany who stood up against great evil, and he learned that the very first way to serve someone is not to talk, not to fix, and not to advise. It is to listen. Before you can help a person, you have to hear them. That sounds simple, but most of us are terrible at it. We listen just long enough to think of what we want to say next.

Listening starts in the quiet place with God. When you are still before God, you practice the skill of listening. You stop talking. You stop rushing. You learn to be fully present. Then, when you walk out into your day, you carry that stillness with you. You listen to the tired mother without checking your phone. You listen to the lonely neighbor without looking at your watch. You listen to the hurting friend without jumping in to fix them.

This is one of the greatest gifts a servant leader can give, because most hurting people do not need your answers. They need your ears. When someone feels truly heard, they feel truly loved. And when you listen well, you often learn what a person really needs, which makes your help ten times better.

This calls us to be better people because listening is love in action. Anyone can talk. It takes a servant's heart to be quiet and pay attention. This week, practice being still with God first, then be still with people. Put the phone down. Look them in the eyes. Let them finish. You will be amazed at how your listening heals hearts, including your own.

Prayer:

Lord, teach me that listening is service. Today, when my friend pours out her troubles, when my child tells me about his day, when my coworker just needs to vent, help me be still and truly listen. Quiet my urge to interrupt with advice or rush to the next thing. "Be still, and know that I am God." Let me live that with people too. I pray for everyone who feels unheard today. Make me the one who stops, sits down, and listens with love. Amen.
