Week 14 · Prayer and Solitude as Fuel for Service

Wednesday – The Longing of the Soul

"Prayer is not asking. It is a longing of the soul." — Mahatma Gandhi
Bible (ESV): Luke 5:16 — "But he would withdraw to desolate places and pray."

Many people think prayer is just a wish list we hand to God. Please fix this. Please give me that. Please help me now. But Gandhi saw something deeper. He said prayer is not really about asking. It is a longing, a deep hunger inside us to be close to something greater than ourselves.

Look at Jesus. The Bible says He "often" went to lonely places to pray. Not once in a while. Often. He was not just asking for things. He was longing to be with His Father. That longing kept His heart soft and His purpose clear. Every time He stepped away from the crowds, He came back with more love for them, not less.

A servant leader who only prays when they need something will burn out fast. But a servant leader who prays because their soul longs for God will stay strong for the long road. That kind of prayer changes you from the inside out. You stop serving to feel good about yourself, and you start serving because love is overflowing from you.

This calls us to be better people because when prayer is a longing and not just asking, we become givers instead of takers. We stop thinking, "What can people do for me?" and start thinking, "How can I pour out what God has poured into me?" The people around you can feel the difference. They can tell when your kindness comes from a full heart or an empty one. Let your soul long for God, and your life will become a gift to everyone near you.

Prayer:

God, my soul is longing for You, not just for answers, but for You. In the middle of my noisy house and my crowded calendar, I feel the pull to withdraw like Jesus did, to find a quiet place and simply be with You. Forgive me for filling every silence with noise. Today, carve out a few still minutes where I can breathe and listen. Satisfy this holy hunger with Your presence. I pray for every restless heart chasing noise. Lead us all to the quiet place where You are waiting. Amen.
