Week 17 · Forgiveness as a Leadership Tool

Friday – Light Despite the Darkness

"Hope is being able to see that there is light despite all of the darkness." — Desmond Tutu
Bible (ESV): Luke 23:34 — "And Jesus said, ‘Father, forgive them, for they know not what they do.’ And they cast lots to divide his garments."

Desmond Tutu lived through apartheid, one of the darkest chapters in his country's history. He saw cruelty, injustice, and hatred up close. Yet he never lost hope, because hope is seeing light even when darkness is all around you. Forgiveness is one of the brightest lights there is.

Look at Jesus on the cross. It does not get darker than that. He was innocent, yet He was beaten, mocked, and nailed to wood. His friends had run away. The crowd was laughing at Him. And in the middle of that darkness, He prayed the most powerful prayer of forgiveness ever spoken: "Father, forgive them, for they know not what they do." He did not wait until the pain stopped. He forgave right in the middle of it.

That is what forgiveness does as a leadership tool. It brings light into the darkest situations. When a team is torn apart by conflict, one person's forgiveness can be the light that starts healing. When a family is broken by old wounds, one person's "I forgive you" can be the dawn after a long night. You do not have to wait until you feel like it. You do not have to wait until they deserve it. Like Jesus, you can forgive in the middle of the pain.

This calls us to be better people because the world is full of darkness, and it is waiting for people who carry light. Every time you forgive, you prove that darkness does not win. You show the people around you that hope is real. Be the light this week. Forgive the unforgivable. Love the unlovable. And watch the darkness run from the light of a forgiving heart.

Prayer:

Father, when Jesus hung on the cross, He prayed for the very people who put Him there. Today, when darkness feels thick, when I have been wronged and no apology is coming, help me see the light anyway. Give me hope that forgiveness is possible even here, even now. I pray for everyone sitting in the dark of bitterness tonight. Shine Your light into their prison. Teach me to pray "forgive them" with my whole heart, and watch the darkness lift. Amen.
