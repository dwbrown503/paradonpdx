Week 23 · The Resurrection: Vindicated Humility

Wednesday – Love Is Stronger Than Hate

"Darkness cannot drive out darkness: only light can do that. Hate cannot drive out hate: only love can do that." — Martin Luther King Jr.
Bible (ESV): John 11:25 — Jesus said to her, "I am the resurrection and the life. Whoever believes in me, though he die, yet shall he live."

The day Jesus died was the darkest day His friends had ever known. They had hoped He would save them, and instead they watched Him suffer. They went home sad and afraid. But three days later, the sun came up on a brand-new morning, and Jesus was alive. The darkest night in history ended in the brightest morning ever. That is what God does. He brings light out of darkness.

Martin Luther King Jr. said that darkness cannot drive out darkness — only light can. Hate cannot drive out hate — only love can. When someone treats us badly, our first thought is often to treat them badly back. We want to argue, get even, or shut them out. But Jesus showed us a better way. From the cross He prayed, "Father, forgive them." After He rose, He did not come back angry. He came back with peace.

Humility is a big part of this. A humble person does not have to win every fight or have the last word. A humble person can let love do the fighting. When a coworker is rude, you can answer with kindness. When a family member is unfair, you can stay calm and keep loving them. That does not mean you let people walk all over you. It means you trust God to be the judge, and you choose to be the light.

Try this in your own life. The next time someone is unkind, pause and ask, "What would love do here?" Maybe it means listening instead of yelling. Maybe it means helping someone who does not deserve it. These small choices push back the darkness. And remember: the resurrection proves that light always wins in the end. Love is stronger than hate, and life is stronger than death.

Prayer:

God, hate looks strong. It shouts, it breaks, it bullies, it wins the argument. But Your love is stronger: it rose from a grave. Today, when hate knocks, in the news, in my neighborhood, in my own heart, answer it with love. Help me bless the person who cursed me and pray for the one who hurt me. I pray for every heart poisoned by hate tonight. Overcome it with Your stronger love. Prove again that light always outlasts darkness. Amen.
