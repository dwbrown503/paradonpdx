Week 4 · The Cultural Context of Ancient Leadership

Wednesday – Giving Without Strings Attached

"The church is the church only when it exists for others." — Dietrich Bonhoeffer (from Letters and Papers from Prison, written in 1944)
Bible (ESV): Matthew 6:1-4 — "'Beware of practicing your righteousness before other people in order to be seen by them, for then you will have no reward from your Father who is in heaven. Thus, when you give to the needy, sound no trumpet before you, as the hypocrites do in the synagogues and in the streets, that they may be praised by others. Truly, I say to you, they have received their reward. But when you give to the needy, do not let your left hand know what your right hand is doing, so that your giving may be in secret. And your Father who sees in secret will reward you.'"

In the ancient world, even kindness came with strings attached. Rich men called patrons gave money, food, and protection to poorer people called clients. But these gifts were really deals. The client had to pay the patron back with loyalty, public praise, and favors. If a patron helped you, everyone expected you to praise his name in the marketplace and support him in return. Giving was a way to buy honor and control.

Jesus taught a completely different way to give. He said, "When you give to the needy, sound no trumpet before you... do not let your left hand know what your right hand is doing, so that your giving may be in secret." No trumpet. No crowd. No applause. Just quiet love, given freely, with no strings attached. The only reward that matters comes from our Father in heaven, who sees in secret.

Dietrich Bonhoeffer put it this way: "The church is the church only when it exists for others." A servant leader does not help people to get something back. He helps because people matter. She gives because love gives. The question is never, "What will this do for me?" The question is always, "What does this person need?"

This is how we live it out. Help someone who can never pay you back. Encourage a person who cannot return the favor. Give your time, your money, or your attention quietly, without telling everyone about it. Do not post it. Do not brag about it. Just do it. When we give without strings, we look like Jesus — and we remind the world that love is not a deal. It is a gift.

Prayer:

Gracious Father, You give to us so freely — sunshine, breath, forgiveness, love we could never earn. Forgive us for giving with strings attached, hoping for a thank-you or a favor in return. Teach us to give the way You give: quietly and freely, with no trumpet and no crowd. Help us notice the coworker who needs a hand but can never pay us back, and meet the need without telling anyone about it. Meet it, walk away quietly, and expect nothing back — just love. Remind us that our lives exist for others, just as Jesus lived for others. May our giving bring You honor and bring hope to hurting people. In Jesus' name we pray. Amen.
