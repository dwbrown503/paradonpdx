Week 25 · The Great Commission: Leading by Multiplying Servants

Tuesday – Everybody Can Serve

"Everybody can be great, because everybody can serve. You don't have to have a college degree to serve. You don't have to make your subject and your verb agree to serve. You only need a heart full of grace, a soul generated by love." — Martin Luther King Jr.
Bible (ESV): Mark 10:45 — "For even the Son of Man came not to be served but to serve, and to give his life as a ransom for many."

Martin Luther King Jr. taught a beautiful truth. You do not need a fancy degree to be great. You do not need perfect grammar or a big title. All you need is a heart full of grace and a soul filled with love. Anybody can serve, so anybody can be great. That is how Jesus led too. He was the King of kings, yet He came to serve instead of being served. He washed His friends' feet. He fed the hungry. He spent time with people nobody else would touch. And He asks us to do the same. When we lead by serving, something powerful happens. We show other people that they can serve too. Some people think, "I am not good enough," or "I do not know enough," or "That job is for someone else." A servant leader gently breaks that lie. They say, "Come with me. I will show you. You can do this." That is how we multiply servants. We do not hold the serving all to ourselves. We hand it out. We invite people in. We let them try, we cheer them on, and we watch them grow. Maybe you help with coffee or communion or greeting at the door. That is a real ministry. And when you teach a new person to do it beside you, you are making room for greatness in them. Every person God made is able to serve in some way. Our job as leaders is to help them see it and believe it. Greatness is not above anyone. It is within anyone who serves with love.

Prayer:

Lord, I do not need a degree, a title, or a perfect past to serve. Anybody can serve, which means I can serve. Today, free me from excuses. My hands can help, my ears can listen, my smile can welcome, my car can drive someone who needs a ride. When I think I am not qualified, remind me that willingness is the only requirement. I pray for everyone who feels too ordinary to matter. Show them they are exactly who You use. Let me serve, starting now. Amen.
