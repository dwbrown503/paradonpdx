Week 5 · The Beatitudes as Leadership Traits (Matthew 5)

Wednesday – The Merciful Leader

"Do your little bit of good where you are; it's those little bits of good put together that overwhelm the world." – Desmond Tutu
Bible (ESV): Matthew 5:7 – "Blessed are the merciful, for they shall receive mercy."

Mercy means being kind to someone who does not deserve it, or forgiving someone who hurt you. It is easy to be kind to people who are kind to us. But a servant leader is merciful even when it is hard. Jesus says that when we show mercy, we will receive mercy too. Think of a time when you made a mistake and someone forgave you instead of punishing you. That gift changed how you felt, right? That is what mercy does. It heals hearts.

Archbishop Tutu lived through hard times in South Africa, where many people were treated badly because of their skin color. When things changed, he helped lead a way of forgiveness instead of revenge. He believed that small acts of goodness, added together, can overcome great evil. You can show mercy today. Forgive the friend who let you down. Be patient with the coworker who is slow to learn. Give food to someone who cannot pay you back. Every little bit of good counts. No act of kindness is too small. A merciful leader builds a team where people are not afraid to make mistakes, because they know they will be treated with grace. That kind of team grows stronger every day, and it changes the world one heart at a time.

Prayer:

Lord, thank You for Your mercy that never runs out. Today help me forgive the friend who broke a promise and be patient with the coworker who is slow to learn a new task. When I want to hold a grudge, remind me how many times You have forgiven me. Show me one small kindness I can do, like a meal for someone who cannot pay me back or a kind word for someone who is struggling. Let all our little bits of good join together to push back the darkness. Make me a leader who heals instead of harms. Amen.
