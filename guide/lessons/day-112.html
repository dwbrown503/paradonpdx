Week 23 · The Resurrection: Vindicated Humility

Tuesday – Risen to Serve

"Do not waste time bothering whether you 'love' your neighbor; act as if you did." — C.S. Lewis
Bible (ESV): Matthew 28:6 — "He is not here, for he has risen, as he said. Come, see the place where he lay."

On the first Easter morning, women went to Jesus' tomb and found it empty. An angel said, "He is not here, for he has risen." Jesus had beaten death. But notice what Jesus did after He rose. He did not go find the leaders who hurt Him and get even. He did not show off His power. Instead, He kept serving. He met Mary Magdalene in the garden and called her by name. He walked with two sad disciples on the road and explained the Scriptures to them. He even cooked breakfast on the beach for His friends and ate with them. The risen King was still a servant.

That tells us something important. The resurrection was not just about Jesus getting His life back. It was about giving us a new kind of life — a life where love shows up in what we do, not just in what we feel. C.S. Lewis said not to waste time wondering if you feel love for your neighbor. Just act as if you do. Feelings can change from day to day, but actions build real love.

What does this look like in real life? It looks like visiting the neighbor who is lonely, even when it is not convenient. It looks like forgiving a family member before they say sorry. It looks like doing the dishes, mowing a lawn, or giving a ride without being asked. These are small acts, but they are resurrection acts — they bring life where there was sadness.

When we serve like this, people start to see Jesus in us. We become living proof that He is alive, because His love is alive in our hands and feet. The empty tomb was the start. Now our serving is the proof.

Prayer:

Lord Jesus, You rose, not to be admired from a distance, but to send us out to serve. Your resurrection gives my ordinary Tuesday a mission. Today, help me act like someone alive: bring hope to the discouraged coworker, energy to the tired volunteer shift, joy to my own kitchen table. When I feel dead inside, resurrect my purpose. I pray for everyone who feels buried by despair. Roll away their stone. Let my risen life serve a risen Lord. Amen.
