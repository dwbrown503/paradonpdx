Week 26 · The Early Church Paradigm (Acts and the Epistles)

Friday – Set Free to Serve

"There are three ways to ultimate success: The first way is to be kind. The second way is to be kind. The third way is to be kind." — Fred Rogers
Bible (ESV): Galatians 5:13 — "For you were called to freedom, brothers. Only do not use your freedom as an opportunity for the flesh, but through love serve one another."

Jesus set us free. That is wonderful news. But freedom is not just about doing whatever we want. Paul told the church in Galatia that their freedom had a purpose: through love serve one another. Think about it this way. If you were locked in a prison and someone opened the door, you would not stay in your cell. You would walk out and live. In the same way, Jesus opened the door of our hearts. Now we are free to love people the way He loves us. Freedom is not a permission slip to be selfish. It is a calling to serve.

Fred Rogers, the kind man from the neighborhood we all remember, said there are three ways to true success: be kind, be kind, be kind. That is the whole secret. The early church understood this. They were free from the old rules and free from fear, so they used their freedom to care for one another. They fed the hungry. They welcomed strangers. They forgave each other. Their kindness was not a show. It was who they had become.

This week we have learned what the early church looked like: they shared everything, they made sure nobody was unseen, their leaders served like shepherds, and they put others first. Now the lesson comes full circle. All of that was possible because they were free — free to love. You are free too. So today, choose kindness on purpose. Smile at someone who is sad. Help someone who cannot pay you back. Speak gently when you could speak harshly. When you serve others humbly in love, you prove what your freedom is for. Be kind, be kind, be kind — and watch God use your kindness to change the world around you.

Prayer:

Heavenly Father, thank You for setting me free through Jesus. I am no longer chained by sin or fear, and I am so grateful. But Lord, do not let me waste my freedom on selfish things. Today, teach me to use it the way You planned, to serve others humbly in love. Make me kind in my words, kind in my actions, kind even when it is hard. When freedom tempts me toward self, turn me toward service. I pray for everyone still in chains tonight. Set them free, and set me free to serve. Amen.
