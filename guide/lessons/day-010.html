Week 2 · The Incarnation and Emptying (Philippians 2)

Friday – Small Hands, Big Good

"Do your little bit of good where you are; it's those little bits of good put together that overwhelm the world." — Desmond Tutu
Bible (ESV): "Therefore God has highly exalted him and bestowed on him the name that is above every name, so that at the name of Jesus every knee should bow, in heaven and on earth and under the earth, and every tongue confess that Jesus Christ is Lord, to the glory of God the Father." (Philippians 2:9–11) "And your Father who sees in secret will reward you." (Matthew 6:4)

Here is the beautiful ending of the story. After Jesus humbled himself all the way down — lower than anyone had ever gone — God lifted him all the way up. "Therefore God exalted him to the highest place." Every knee will bow. Every tongue will confess that Jesus Christ is Lord.

Do you see the pattern? Jesus went down, and God brought him up. This is how God's kingdom works: those who humble themselves will be lifted up. We do not lift ourselves. We do not fight for the top. We go low in love, and God does the lifting.

Desmond Tutu understood this. He helped bring down apartheid in South Africa — not with armies, but with courage, truth, and love. He said, "Do your little bit of good where you are; it's those little bits of good put together that overwhelm the world."

Notice he did not say, "Do a huge, famous thing." He said, "Do your little bit." Bring the soup to the sick neighbor. Share your lunch with the lonely kid. Write the thank-you note. Forgive the small hurt. Apologize first. Little bits of good, done every day, pile up until they overwhelm the darkness.

Leaders sometimes wait for the big moment — the big speech, the big project, the big stage. But Jesus' greatness came from daily obedience, one act of love at a time. He washed feet. He touched lepers. He ate with outcasts. Small hands. Big good.

And here is the promise: God sees what you do in secret. The kindness nobody notices. The sacrifice nobody applauds. God sees, and God honors it. You do not need a stage. You do not need a title. You only need willing hands and a loving heart.

You have walked through the mind of Christ this week: he laid down his glory, he became a servant, he lived for others, he poured out his life, and God lifted him high. Now it is your turn. Go and live it — one little bit of good at a time.

Prayer:

Lord Jesus, we end this week in awe of You. You went down so we could be lifted up. Teach us Your pattern: go low in love, and let You do the lifting. Show us our little bit of good today — the soup carried to the sick neighbor, the lunch shared with the lonely kid, the thank-you note written, the small hurt forgiven. Help us do these things quietly, without telling everyone or posting about them, knowing You see every secret kindness and will honor it. We do not need a stage. We only need willing hands and loving hearts. Fill our world with little bits of good until Your love overwhelms the darkness. Amen.
