Week 3 · Secure Identity as a Prerequisite for Service

Thursday – Small Things, Great Love

"Don't look for big things, just do small things with great love." — Mother Teresa
Bible (ESV): "Whatever you do, work heartily, as for the Lord and not for men, knowing that from the Lord you will receive the inheritance as your reward. You are serving the Lord Christ." — Colossians 3:23-24

Mother Teresa spent her life caring for the poorest people in the streets of Calcutta, India. She washed wounds. She held the hands of dying strangers. She did work that most people would never notice and never praise. Her advice was simple: "Don't look for big things, just do small things with great love."

That is a hard teaching, because our world worships big things. Big stages. Big crowds. Big numbers. When we serve, part of us wants it to be big enough to matter. But Mother Teresa is telling us that the size of the act is not what makes it great — the size of the love behind it is. A small act done with great love can change a person's whole day, and sometimes their whole life.

The Bible says the same thing: "Whatever you do, work heartily, as for the Lord and not for men, knowing that from the Lord you will receive the inheritance as your reward. You are serving the Lord Christ." Did you catch that last part? When you serve your neighbor, you are actually serving Christ Himself. Jesus said that when we feed the hungry, welcome the stranger, or visit the lonely, it is as if we did it for Him.

Think about what this means for the small, unseen jobs. Setting out chairs before church. Brewing the coffee. Wiping down tables. Calling a friend who is hurting. None of these will ever make the news. But done with great love, each one is done for Jesus Himself.

A secure heart does not need credit. It does not need a thank-you card or a round of applause, because it is already full. And a full heart can pour out freely. This week, do one small thing with great love — and do it only for God.

Prayer:

Jesus, thank You for seeing the small things I do when no one else does. Forgive me for chasing big moments instead of loving well in small ones. Teach me Mother Teresa's secret: that a tiny act done with great love is never tiny to You. When I brew the coffee, set out the chairs, or wipe down the tables, remind me that I am serving You — that when I feed the hungry or sit with the lonely, it is as if I did it for You. Fill my ordinary tasks with Your extraordinary love, and let my quiet faithfulness bring You joy. In Your holy name, amen.
