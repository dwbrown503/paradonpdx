paragonpdx Servant Leadership Guide

130 Days of Learning to Lead by Serving. One lesson per day, Monday through Friday, across 26 weeks.

Week 1: The Prophetic Profile (Isaiah's Servant Songs)

Long before Jesus was born, the prophet Isaiah painted a picture of the kind of leader God was sending. This Servant would not shout or push people around. He would be gentle with the hurting — He would not break a bruised reed or snuff out a small flame. This week you will meet that Servant up close, and you will see why Martin Luther King Jr. said anybody can be great, because anybody can serve. The journey starts here: greatness comes through serving.

  • Day 1: Monday – Greatness Comes Through Serving
  • Day 2: Tuesday – Called and Chosen for a Purpose
  • Day 3: Wednesday – Steady When the Road Is Hard
  • Day 4: Thursday – Carrying the Pain of Others
  • Day 5: Friday – The Servant Who Looks Like Jesus

    • Looking back: This week you saw God's Servant the way Isaiah saw Him — chosen, gentle, and strong enough to bring justice to the whole world. You learned that you are called and chosen for a purpose, that steady faith holds up when the road gets hard, and that a true servant carries the pain of others. The Servant Isaiah described looks exactly like Jesus. Next week, you will watch that Servant lay everything down.

Week 2: The Incarnation and Emptying (Philippians 2)

Jesus had everything — He was in the very form of God. And He laid it all down. This week walks through Philippians 2, where Paul shows us a King who emptied Himself and took the form of a servant. You will learn what it means to choose the low place on purpose, to live for others instead of yourself, and to lift people up the way Jesus lifted us. Even small hands can do big good when they serve.

  • Day 6: Monday – The Choice to Lay It Down
  • Day 7: Tuesday – Greatness Through Serving
  • Day 8: Wednesday – Living for Others
  • Day 9: Thursday – A Life That Lifts Others
  • Day 10: Friday – Small Hands, Big Good

    • Looking back: This week you watched Jesus lay down His glory and pick up a servant's towel. You learned that greatness comes through serving, that living for others is the whole point, and that a life poured out lifts everyone around it. Even the smallest act of kindness carries big weight in God's hands. Next week, you will find out why only a secure heart can truly serve.

Week 3: Secure Identity as a Prerequisite for Service

Before Jesus did a single miracle, His Father said, ‘This is my beloved Son, with whom I am well pleased.’ He was loved before He ever lifted a finger. This week you will learn why that matters for you: you cannot give away what you do not have. A leader who knows they are loved does not serve to earn love — they serve because they are already full. When your heart is secure, your hands are free to be kind without keeping score.

  • Day 11: Monday – Loved Before You Ever Lift a Finger
  • Day 12: Tuesday – Kindness Without Keeping Score
  • Day 13: Wednesday – Ordinary People, God-Sized Purpose
  • Day 14: Thursday – Small Things, Great Love
  • Day 15: Friday – Secure Enough to Kneel

    • Looking back: This week you learned the secret underneath all serving: you are loved before you ever lift a finger. A secure leader gives kindness without keeping score, trusts ordinary days with God-sized purpose, and does small things with great love. Secure enough to kneel — that is the goal. Next week, you will see how Jesus turned the whole world's idea of leadership upside down.

Week 4: The Cultural Context of Ancient Leadership

In Jesus' day, leaders ruled from above. They used power to get honor, and everybody played the honor game. Then Jesus said, ‘It shall not be so among you.’ This week you will step into that ancient world and feel how shocking His words were. You will learn the difference between power that grabs and strength that kneels — and you will end the week at the basin, watching Jesus pick up a towel.

  • Day 16: Monday – Power That Rules from Above
  • Day 17: Tuesday – The Honor Game
  • Day 18: Wednesday – Giving Without Strings Attached
  • Day 19: Thursday – Strength That Kneels
  • Day 20: Friday – The Towel and the Basin

    • Looking back: This week you saw how the world does leadership — power from above, honor chased, strings attached to every gift. And you saw Jesus flip it: whoever would be great must be a servant. Strength that kneels is stronger than power that struts. You ended at the towel and the basin. Next week, Jesus' own words in Matthew 5 will show you what a servant leader looks like from the inside out.

Week 5: The Beatitudes as Leadership Traits (Matthew 5)

Jesus sat down on a mountainside and described the kind of people God blesses — the meek, the merciful, the peacemakers, the pure in heart. The world calls these people weak. Jesus calls them leaders. This week you will walk through the Beatitudes one by one and find out why meekness is strength under control, why hungering for what is right changes everything, and why mercy and peacemaking are leadership superpowers.

  • Day 21: Monday – Meekness: Strength Under Control
  • Day 22: Tuesday – Hungry for What Is Right
  • Day 23: Wednesday – The Merciful Leader
  • Day 24: Thursday – The Peacemaker
  • Day 25: Friday – Pure Hearts See Clearly

    • Looking back: This week the Beatitudes became your leadership manual. Meekness is strength under control. Hunger for righteousness keeps a leader honest. Mercy, peacemaking, and a pure heart are not soft extras — they are the core of the job. Blessed are the servant leaders. Next week, Jesus makes it even plainer: the first shall be last.

Week 6: The First Shall Be Last (Mark 9 & 10)

The disciples argued about who was the greatest. Jesus sat down, called them close, and said, ‘If anyone would be first, he must be last of all and servant of all.’ Then He took a child in His arms. This week you will sit in on that conversation. You will learn why greatness is service, why the last shall be first, and why childlike humility opens doors that pride keeps shut. And you will see the ransom — love that pays the highest price.

  • Day 26: Monday – Greatness Is Service
  • Day 27: Tuesday – The Last Shall Be First
  • Day 28: Wednesday – The Ransom Paid in Love
  • Day 29: Thursday – Childlike Humility
  • Day 30: Friday – Strength to Forgive

    • Looking back: This week you heard it straight from Jesus: greatness is service, and the last shall be first. You learned that childlike humility is not thinking less of yourself but thinking of yourself less, that love pays the ransom price willingly, and that strength shows up as forgiveness. The world's ladder is upside down in God's kingdom. Next week, you will learn what it means to be a faithful steward of everything God puts in your hands.

Week 7: The Parable of the Faithful Steward

A steward does not own the house — he takes care of it for the master. Jesus told a story about a faithful manager, and this week you will live inside that story. You will learn why faithfulness with little leads to trust with much, why much given means much required, and why we work for God and not for people's applause. Your gifts were never meant to sit on a shelf. They were meant to be given.

  • Day 31: Monday – Faithful With Little
  • Day 32: Tuesday – Much Is Given, Much Required
  • Day 33: Wednesday – Working for God, Not People
  • Day 34: Thursday – Ready When the Master Returns
  • Day 35: Friday – Gifts Meant to Be Given

    • Looking back: This week you learned the steward's mindset: everything you have is on loan from God. Faithful with little, trusted with much. Work for God, not for people. Stay ready, because the Master is returning. And remember — your gifts were meant to be given, not kept. Next week, you will follow the Shepherd who leaves the ninety-nine to find the one.

Week 8: Seeking the Lost vs. Seeking Status

Religious leaders in Jesus' day chased status. Jesus chased lost sheep. This week you will walk the hills with the Shepherd who leaves the ninety-nine to find the one. You will learn why status will never save you, why ‘the least of these’ matter most to God, and why leaving what is comfortable to find one hurting person is the heart of servant leadership. If you cannot feed a hundred, feed one.

  • Day 36: Monday – The Shepherd Who Searches
  • Day 37: Tuesday – The Least of These
  • Day 38: Wednesday – Status Will Not Save You
  • Day 39: Thursday – Leaving the Comfortable to Find the One
  • Day 40: Friday – If You Can't Feed a Hundred, Feed One

    • Looking back: This week you chose the Shepherd's path over the status game. You learned that God measures a life by the difference it makes, that the least of these are the greatest to Him, and that one person found is worth leaving the comfortable behind. Feed one. Find one. Love one. Next week, you will go where it hurts — to the untouchables Jesus touched.

Week 9: Touching the Untouchable (Leper Cleansing)

Nobody touched a leper. It was the rule. Then Jesus, moved with pity, stretched out His hand and touched him. This week you will learn what compassion really costs: it asks us to go where it hurts and enter into places of pain. You will put down your stones of judgment, roll up your sleeves, and discover that thankful hearts grow in healed lives. We can love because we were first loved.

  • Day 41: Monday – Going Where It Hurts
  • Day 42: Tuesday – Putting Down Our Stones
  • Day 43: Wednesday – Thankful Hearts, Healed Lives
  • Day 44: Thursday – Rolling Up Our Sleeves
  • Day 45: Friday – Loved So We Can Love

    • Looking back: This week you watched Jesus touch the untouchable — and you learned to do the same. Go where it hurts. Put down your stones; if you judge people, you have no time to love them. Roll up your sleeves and serve. Thankful hearts grow where healing happens. Loved, so we can love. Next week, you will see how Jesus lifted up the people His culture pushed down — women and children.

Week 10: Elevating Women and Children in Ancient Culture

In Jesus' day, women and children were pushed to the edges. The disciples tried to shoo the children away. Jesus said, ‘Let them come to me.’ This week you will see Jesus welcome the overlooked, listen like Mary, and declare that in Christ we are all one. You will learn that how you make people feel matters, that love is something you act out, and that the smallest person in the room may be the greatest teacher.

  • Day 46: Monday – "Let Them Come"
  • Day 47: Tuesday – "How You Make Them Feel"
  • Day 48: Wednesday – "Act Like Love"
  • Day 49: Thursday – "Listening Like Mary"
  • Day 50: Friday – "All One in Christ"

    • Looking back: This week you watched Jesus open His arms to women and children — the very people His culture dismissed. Let them come. How you make people feel is how they will remember you. Act like love, listen like Mary, and remember we are all one in Christ. The overlooked are never overlooked by God. Next week, the walls come down — Jesus crosses every dividing line.

Week 11: Crossing Racial Boundaries (The Samaritan Woman)

Jews and Samaritans did not talk. Men did not talk to strange women at wells. Jesus did both — and changed a town. This week you will tear down the wall with Him. You will meet the neighbor on the road, learn that God shows no favorites, hear good news for the oppressed, and see power used for good instead of for control. Servant leaders do not protect dividing lines. They cross them.

  • Day 51: Monday – "Tearing Down the Wall"
  • Day 52: Tuesday – "The Neighbor on the Road"
  • Day 53: Wednesday – "No Favorites With God"
  • Day 54: Thursday – "Good News for the Oppressed"
  • Day 55: Friday – "Power Used for Good"

    • Looking back: This week you crossed every line with Jesus — racial, cultural, religious. The wall came down. The Samaritan on the road became the hero of the story. No favorites with God. Good news for the oppressed. Power used for good, not for control. A servant leader builds bridges, not walls. Next week, you will watch a boy's small lunch feed a multitude.

Week 12: Feeding the Multitudes (Shared Resources)

Five barley loaves and two fish. That was all a boy had — and Jesus fed thousands with it. This week you will learn the math of God's kingdom: small gifts plus big love equals more than enough. You will give with a happy heart, share like family, and find out that when God's people share, there is enough for everyone. What is in your hands right now? It may be somebody's miracle.

  • Day 56: Monday – A Boy's Small Lunch
  • Day 57: Tuesday – Giving With a Happy Heart
  • Day 58: Wednesday – Enough for Everyone
  • Day 59: Thursday – Sharing Like Family
  • Day 60: Friday – Hungry? Feed Them

    • Looking back: This week a boy's small lunch became a feast. You learned to give with a happy heart — not reluctantly, not under pressure — and to share like family. In God's economy there is enough for everyone, and leftovers besides. Hungry? Feed them. Your little bit, given with love, is never little to God. Next week, Jesus picks up a whip of cords — and you will learn when a servant leader must say ‘enough.’

Week 13: Rebuking Religious Exploitation (Cleansing the Temple)

Jesus walked into the temple, saw people being cheated in God's name, and drove the money changers out. ‘My house shall be called a house of prayer for all nations.’ This week you will learn that servant leadership is not always soft — sometimes love cleans house. You will clear out the clutter, protect prayer, and stand up when religion is used to exploit people. The greatest among you will be your servant — and sometimes a servant must confront.

  • Day 61: Monday – Cleaning Out the Clutter
  • Day 62: Tuesday – A House of Prayer
  • Day 63: Wednesday – The Greatest Will Serve
  • Day 64: Thursday – Learning to Do Right
  • Day 65: Friday – For All Nations

    • Looking back: This week you saw the other side of Jesus' gentleness: righteous courage. He cleaned out the clutter, defended the house of prayer, and stood with the exploited against the exploiters. A servant leader does not look away from wrong — love confronts what harms people. For all nations, for all people. Next week, you will learn where Jesus got His strength: quiet time alone with the Father.

Week 14: Prayer and Solitude as Fuel for Service

Before the crowds, before the miracles, Jesus rose very early while it was still dark and went to a lonely place to pray. This week you will learn the rhythm behind all His serving: quiet first, service second. You will bring your small worries to a big God, listen until your soul settles, and take one step of faith at a time. Listening is the first service — and prayer is the fuel that keeps a servant going.

  • Day 66: Monday – Quiet First, Service Second
  • Day 67: Tuesday – Small Worries Belong in Big Prayers
  • Day 68: Wednesday – The Longing of the Soul
  • Day 69: Thursday – One Step of Faith
  • Day 70: Friday – Listening Is the First Service

    • Looking back: This week you learned the servant's secret fuel: prayer and solitude. Quiet first, service second. Small worries belong in big prayers. Take one step of faith, then another. And remember — listening is the first service you can offer anyone. A leader who never stops to pray will eventually run on empty. Next week, you will learn how to stand steady when criticism comes and popularity fades.

Week 15: Handling Criticism and Popularity

Jesus was cheered one day and jeered the next. Crowds loved Him, leaders plotted against Him — and He never wavered. This week you will learn how to handle both criticism and popularity without losing yourself. You will stop seeking people's approval and start seeking God's, let go of worry that steals today's strength, and lose yourself in service. The time is always right to do what is right — no matter who is watching.

  • Day 71: Monday – The Time Is Always Right
  • Day 72: Tuesday – Worry Steals Today's Strength
  • Day 73: Wednesday – Impossible Until It's Done
  • Day 74: Thursday – Feed Just One
  • Day 75: Friday – Lose Yourself in Service

    • Looking back: This week you learned to stand steady in the storm of opinions. Am I seeking the approval of people or of God? Worry steals today's strength — prayer gives it back. Feed just one. Lose yourself in service and you will find yourself. Criticism cannot sink a leader anchored in God. Next week, you will learn to hold truth and grace together the way Jesus did — full of both, never one without the other.

Week 16: Inhabiting Truth and Grace

‘And the Word became flesh and dwelt among us... full of grace and truth.’ Jesus never chose between honesty and kindness — He carried both, fully. This week you will learn to do the same. You will look at what people suffer before you judge them, trade judgment for love, and do your little bit of good where you are. Truth without grace crushes. Grace without truth drifts. A servant leader holds both.

  • Day 76: Monday – Look at What They Suffer
  • Day 77: Tuesday – No Time to Judge
  • Day 78: Wednesday – Your Little Bit of Good
  • Day 79: Thursday – A Threat to Justice Everywhere
  • Day 80: Friday – Love Until It Hurts

    • Looking back: This week you learned to be full of grace and truth, like Jesus. Look at what people suffer. Put down judgment — if you judge people, you have no time to love them. Do your little bit of good where you are; those little bits put together overwhelm the world. Love until it hurts. Next week, you will pick up the hardest tool in a leader's hands: forgiveness.

Week 17: Forgiveness as a Leadership Tool

Every leader gets hurt. People will fail you, betray you, and let you down. What you do next decides what kind of leader you are. This week you will learn that forgiveness is not weakness — it is the key that unlocks the door of resentment and the handcuffs of hatred. You will stick with love, stay faithful in small things, and let your light shine despite the darkness. Forgiven people forgive.

  • Day 81: Monday – The Key That Unlocks
  • Day 82: Tuesday – Stick With Love
  • Day 83: Wednesday – Taught to Love
  • Day 84: Thursday – Faithful in Small Things
  • Day 85: Friday – Light Despite the Darkness

    • Looking back: This week you learned that forgiveness is a leadership tool, not a feeling. It is the key that unlocks resentment. Stick with love — hate is too great a burden to bear. Be faithful in small things, and let your light shine even in dark places. As God forgave you, forgive others. Next week, you will kneel with Jesus in the Upper Room and watch greatness wear a towel.

Week 18: The Upper Room and the Basin (John 13)

It was the night before the cross. Jesus knew everything was in His hands — and He got up, wrapped a towel around His waist, and washed His friends' feet. This week you will kneel beside that basin. You will learn that greatness wears a towel, that small acts carry great love, and that we were built for others. ‘If I then, your Lord and Teacher, have washed your feet, you also ought to wash one another's feet.’

  • Day 86: Monday – Greatness Wears a Towel
  • Day 87: Tuesday – Small Acts, Great Love
  • Day 88: Wednesday – Looking Up, Not Down
  • Day 89: Thursday – Losing Yourself to Find Yourself
  • Day 90: Friday – Built for Others

    • Looking back: This week you knelt at the basin in the Upper Room. Greatness wears a towel. Small acts, done with great love, change everything. Look up, not down — lose yourself to find yourself. You were built for others, called to freedom so you can serve in love. Next week, the night grows darker: you will follow Jesus into the garden of Gethsemane.

Week 19: Gethsemane: Submission to the Father's Will

In the garden, Jesus fell on His face and prayed, ‘Father, if you are willing, remove this cup from me. Nevertheless, not my will, but yours, be done.’ This week you will sit with Him in His hardest hour. You will learn what submission really looks like — weak body, willing spirit — and how to take one step in the dark when you cannot see the way. Courage with trembling hands still counts as courage.

  • Day 91: Monday – Love Deeper Than the Pit
  • Day 92: Tuesday – The Painful Best
  • Day 93: Wednesday – One Step in the Dark
  • Day 94: Thursday – Weak Body, Willing Spirit
  • Day 95: Friday – Courage With Trembling Hands

    • Looking back: This week you knelt with Jesus in Gethsemane. You saw love deeper than the pit and learned that the painful best is still the best. Weak body, willing spirit — take one step in the dark and trust the Father. Courage with trembling hands is still courage. ‘Not my will, but yours.’ Next week, you will stand with Jesus in the trials — silent under false accusation.

Week 20: The Trials: Silence Under False Accusation

They lied about Him. They struck Him. They mocked Him. And Jesus remained silent. ‘When he was reviled, he did not revile in return; when he suffered, he did not threaten.’ This week you will learn the hardest lesson of all: no payback. You will drop the poison of revenge, fight hate with love, and find courage at the testing point. The silent Lamb shows us that true strength does not need to shout.

  • Day 96: Monday – No Payback
  • Day 97: Tuesday – Courage at the Testing Point

  • Day 99: Thursday – Fighting Hate With Love
  • Day 100: Friday – The Silent Lamb

    • Looking back: This week you stood with Jesus through the trials — and He never fought back. No payback. Drop the poison of bitterness before it poisons you. Fight hate with love. Courage at the testing point looks like silence, not shouting. The Lamb was silent, and His silence shook the world. Next week, you will stand at the foot of the cross.

Week 21: The Crucifixion: Forgiving from the Cross

Nails in His hands, thorns on His head, mockers at His feet — and Jesus prayed, ‘Father, forgive them, for they know not what they do.’ This week you will stand at the cross and learn the deepest lesson of servant leadership: forgive anyway. You will find the power to forgive, the strength to forgive when it is hard, and the love that changes enemies into brothers. Forgiveness is an act of the will — and the will can choose.

  • Day 101: Monday – Forgive Anyway
  • Day 102: Tuesday – The Power to Forgive
  • Day 103: Wednesday – Strong Enough to Forgive
  • Day 104: Thursday – When Forgiving Is Hard
  • Day 105: Friday – Love That Changes Enemies

Find the power to forgive even when it is hard — because he who is devoid of the power to forgive is devoid of the power to love. Love that forgiving changes enemies. Next week, you will learn what that death purchased: the ransom paid, the ultimate service.

Week 22: The Ransom Paid: Death as Ultimate Service

‘For even the Son of Man came not to be served but to serve, and to give his life as a ransom for many.’ This week you will face the full weight of those words. Jesus did not just serve — He gave His life. You will learn what it means to die to self daily, to put love into every act, and to lead from behind like a shepherd rather than strut in front like a king. ‘Greater love has no one than this, that someone lay down his life for his friends.’

  • Day 106: Monday – Called to Die to Self
  • Day 107: Tuesday – How Much Love We Put In
  • Day 108: Wednesday – A Life That Counts
  • Day 109: Thursday – Report for Duty
  • Day 110: Friday – Leading From Behind

    • Looking back: This week you stood before the ransom. Called to die to self — not once, but daily. It is not how much we do, but how much love we put in. A life that counts is a life poured out. Report for duty, and lead from behind. The cross is the ultimate service. Next week, the stone rolls away — vindicated humility rises.

Week 23: The Resurrection: Vindicated Humility

They humbled Him to the grave. God lifted Him to glory. ‘He humbled himself by becoming obedient to the point of death... therefore God has highly exalted him.’ This week you will celebrate the resurrection — proof that humility wins in the end. You will learn that God lifts up the lowly, that love is stronger than hate, and that small kindnesses add up to a big victory. He is risen — so rise and serve.

Risen to serve: the resurrection is not just a doctrine, it is a marching order. Love is stronger than hate, and small kindnesses win big victories. Rise again, every morning. Next week, you will walk a beach with Jesus as He restores a fallen leader.

Week 24: Restoring the Fallen Leader (Peter on the Beach)

Peter denied Jesus three times. Then, at dawn on a beach, Jesus cooked him breakfast and asked three times, ‘Do you love me?’ This week you will learn that failure is not final. You will watch Jesus restore a fallen leader gently — no shame, no lecture, just love and a new assignment: ‘Feed my sheep.’ If you have ever failed, this week is for you. And it will teach you to restore others the same way.

  • Day 116: Monday – Jesus Comes to the Shore

Week 25: The Great Commission: Leading by Multiplying Servants

‘Go therefore and make disciples of all nations.’ Jesus' final order was not ‘gather followers’ — it was ‘make makers.’ This week you will learn the highest form of servant leadership: multiplying yourself. The commission is to make makers. Everybody can serve, and everybody can teach somebody else to serve. The shepherd leads from behind, and little bits of good, put together, overwhelm the world.

Next week, you will see it all come alive in the early church.

They shared everything they had. Nobody was left unseen. They chose shepherds, not kings. This final week you will step into the book of Acts and watch servant leadership become a way of life for a whole community. ‘And all who believed were together and had all things in common.’ You have walked 130 days — from Isaiah's Servant to the early church. Now go live it: set free to serve.
