Mindset & Well-being

Finding Peace and Renewing Your Mind

This is a 4-week study written for women of all ages, built on biblical principles for overcoming anxious or toxic thoughts, finding real peace, and renewing your mind day by day.

Each week carries one theme. Each day brings a short devotional, a prayer, and questions to work through — the same simple question format as the Servant Leadership study. Sundays are for gratitude: a quiet reflection on being intentional with what God is doing in you.

The four weeks

  • 2Peacefultrading worry for trust
  • 3Joyouschoosing joy on purpose
  • 4Reneweda made-new mind

How it works

  • 24 lessons — 6 days a week, Monday through Saturday
  • A daily devotional, a unique prayer, and reflection questions
  • A Sunday gratitude reflection each week
