Week 3 · Secure Identity as a Prerequisite for Service

Monday – Loved Before You Ever Lift a Finger

"There is no pit so deep that God's love is not deeper still." — Corrie ten Boom, The Hiding Place
Bible (ESV): "And behold, a voice from heaven said, 'This is my beloved Son, with whom I am well pleased.'" — Matthew 3:17. "We love because he first loved us." — 1 John 4:19

Before Jesus ever preached a sermon, healed a sick person, or fed a hungry crowd, something beautiful happened. At His baptism, the sky opened and God the Father spoke: "This is my beloved Son, with whom I am well pleased." Think about that. Jesus had not done a single miracle yet. He had earned nothing. And God was already proud of Him. God gave Jesus His love and His approval first — before any work, before any success, before anyone was watching.

That is exactly how God treats you. Your worth does not come from how much you do. It comes from how deeply you are loved. The Bible says, "We love because he first loved us." He loved you first — not after you proved yourself, not when you got your life together, but first. Your identity was settled before you ever lifted a finger.

So many of us serve backwards. We say yes to everything, hoping people will notice. Hoping we will feel important. Hoping God will finally be pleased. But serving to earn love is like pouring water into a cup with a hole in the bottom. It never stays full. You end up tired, hurt, and empty, always chasing the next thank-you.

Corrie ten Boom learned this in the darkest place imaginable — a Nazi prison camp. Everything was taken from her: her freedom, her family, her comfort. And there, in that pit, she discovered that God's love goes deeper still. Nothing could take it away, because she never earned it in the first place.

When you know you are already loved, serving changes completely. You do not need applause. You can do the quiet job nobody sees and still feel joy, because your heart is already full. That is what it means to serve from a secure heart. Rest today in this simple truth: you are God's child, and He is pleased with you.

Prayer:

Dear Father, thank You for loving me first — before I preached, healed, or served, before I did a single thing for You. When I look at myself, You see Your child, and You are pleased. This morning, when I am tempted to say yes to everything just to feel important, remind me that I do not have to earn Your love. When my heart chases the next thank-you, fill it with You instead. Teach me to serve from the love I already have — to love freely, kindly, and without keeping score. Let my cup overflow from Yours, so love spills out on everyone around me. I pray in the name of Jesus, amen.
