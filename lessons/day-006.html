Week 2 · The Incarnation and Emptying (Philippians 2)

Monday – The Choice to Lay It Down

"If you are humble nothing will touch you, neither praise nor disgrace, because you know what you are." — Mother Teresa
Bible (ESV): "who, though he was in the form of God, did not count equality with God a thing to be grasped, but emptied himself, by taking the form of a servant, being born in the likeness of men." (Philippians 2:6–7)

Picture the throne of heaven. That is where Jesus was. He was in the form of God — all power, all glory, all praise, forever.

But he did not hold onto it. He did not use his position to gain more for himself. Instead, he emptied himself. He laid down his crown and stepped into our world. He took the form of a servant. He was born in the likeness of men — flesh and blood, hunger and thirst, tears and tiredness — just like you and me.

Why does this matter for leaders? Because most leaders do the opposite. Most leaders climb to get more: more praise, more power, more comfort. Jesus climbed down. True servant leadership starts with this choice: laying down what you could claim, so that others can rise.

Mother Teresa lived this. She could have lived a comfortable life, but she chose to serve the poorest of the poor. And she said something wise: "If you are humble nothing will touch you, neither praise nor disgrace, because you know what you are." When you know who you are in God — deeply loved, nothing more and nothing less — you are free. Praise does not make your head big. Criticism does not break your heart. You do not have to prove anything to anyone.

So what does laying it down look like in real life? It looks like the manager who stays late to help clean up after the meeting instead of leaving it for others. It looks like the parent who says, "I was wrong, please forgive me," to their own child. It looks like the team captain who does the hardest drill right beside the weakest player. It looks like you, this week, taking the low task nobody wants and doing it with joy.

A leader who has laid down their ego leads from peace, not pride. And people feel safe around a leader like that. They grow. They heal. They begin to serve others too. That is how Jesus changes the world — one laid-down life at a time.

Prayer:

Lord Jesus, You had everything — all glory, all power — yet You laid it all down and took the form of a servant. Teach us to lay down our pride too. When we want to win an argument at home and prove we are right, help us let it go. When we are tempted to take credit at work, give us the grace to share it instead. Remind us that we are deeply loved by You, so praise does not puff us up and criticism does not break us. Today, show us the low task nobody wants — and help us do it with joy, the way You would. Amen.
