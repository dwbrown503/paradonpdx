Week 6 · The First Shall Be Last (Mark 9 & 10)

Thursday – Childlike Humility

"The church is the church only when it exists for others... not dominating, but helping and serving." – Dietrich Bonhoeffer
Bible (ESV): Mark 10:14-15 – "But when Jesus saw it, he was indignant and said to them, ‘Let the children come to me; do not hinder them, for to such belongs the kingdom of God. Truly, I say to you, whoever does not receive the kingdom of God like a child shall not enter it.’"

One day, people brought little children to Jesus, and the disciples tried to send them away. They thought Jesus was too important for children. But Jesus was upset with the disciples. He said the kingdom of God belongs to people like these little ones. Children do not pretend to be important. They do not care about titles or status. They come with open hands and trusting hearts. Jesus says that is how we must come to God, and that is how a leader must serve.

Dietrich Bonhoeffer said the church only matters when it exists for others, not for itself. He meant that a group of people is not great because of its big buildings or famous leaders. It is great when it helps and serves. The same is true for any leader. You are not great because of your position. You are great when you help others. Think like a child: do not worry about looking important. Just be honest, be kind, and be ready to share. A childlike leader asks simple questions, admits when they are wrong, and celebrates when others succeed. They do not try to control everyone. They serve everyone. When you lead with the humble heart of a child, people feel loved instead of used, and your leadership points them straight to Jesus.

Prayer:

Heavenly Father, make me like a little child in my heart. Take away my need to look important and my fear of looking foolish. I pray for all the children of the world, the hungry ones, the scared ones, the ones who feel alone. Send kind people into their lives and protect them. Teach me to welcome everyone the way Jesus welcomed the children, with open arms and no rushing. Help our churches and our teams exist for others, not for ourselves. Give me a humble heart that serves with joy. Amen.
