Week 6 · The First Shall Be Last (Mark 9 & 10)

Friday – Strength to Forgive

"The weak can never forgive. Forgiveness is the attribute of the strong." – Mahatma Gandhi
Bible (ESV): Mark 10:27 – "Jesus looked at them and said, ‘With man it is impossible, but not with God. For all things are possible with God.’"

Forgiving someone who hurt you is one of the hardest things in the world. Many people think forgiving means you are weak, like you are letting the other person win. Gandhi said the opposite. He said only the strong can forgive. It takes real strength to let go of anger and choose peace instead. Jesus agreed. When His disciples wondered how anyone could be saved, He told them that with God, all things are possible — including forgiving the unforgivable.

A servant leader must be a forgiving leader. If you hold on to every hurt, your heart gets heavy, and you cannot lead with love. Think of a leader who refuses to forgive. Their team walks on eggshells, afraid of making mistakes. Now think of a leader who forgives. Their team feels safe to try, to learn, and to grow. That is the kind of leader Jesus was. On the cross, He forgave the very people who hurt Him. You can start small. Forgive the driver who cut you off. Forgive the friend who forgot your birthday. Forgive the family member who said something mean. Each time you forgive, you grow stronger, not weaker. And with God's help, even the hardest forgiveness becomes possible. A forgiving leader sets people free — including themselves.

Prayer:

God, I admit that forgiving is hard for me. I hold on to hurt because letting go feels like losing. But You tell me that with You, all things are possible. Today, help me release the anger I carry toward the person who wronged me, at work, in my family, or long ago. I pray for everyone carrying the heavy weight of unforgiveness. Set them free, and set me free too. Teach me that forgiveness is not weakness. It is the greatest strength of all. Make me strong enough to forgive like Jesus forgave. Amen.
