Week 6 · The First Shall Be Last (Mark 9 & 10)

Wednesday – The Ransom Paid in Love

"There is no pit so deep, that God's love is not deeper still." – Corrie ten Boom
Bible (ESV): Mark 10:45 – "For even the Son of Man came not to be served but to serve, and to give his life as a ransom for many."

A ransom is the price paid to set someone free. Jesus said He came to give His life as a ransom for many. That means He paid the highest price possible — His own life — to set us free. He did not serve us halfway. He served us all the way to the cross. This is the deepest kind of servant leadership. It is love that costs something real.

Corrie ten Boom knew about deep pits. She and her family hid Jewish people from danger during World War II, and she was sent to a prison camp for it. She lost her sister there. But she discovered that even in the darkest place, God's love was deeper. She forgave the guards who hurt her, and she spent the rest of her life telling the world about God's love. A servant leader does not quit when serving gets hard. When helping people costs you time, comfort, or even safety, you keep going, because love is worth it. Most of us will never face what Corrie faced. But we all face small chances to pay a price for others: giving up our free time to help a friend, spending our money on someone in need, or forgiving someone who does not deserve it. Every time we choose to pay that price, we look a little more like Jesus, who paid it all.

Prayer:

Lord Jesus, thank You for paying the ransom for us with Your own life. There is no pit so deep that Your love cannot reach. I pray for people who feel trapped today, in prison, in addiction, in deep sadness, or in fear. Let them feel Your love reaching down to them right where they are. Give me courage to keep serving even when it costs me something real, like my time, my comfort, or my money. Teach me to forgive when it is hard and to love when it hurts. Make my life a gift to others. Amen.
